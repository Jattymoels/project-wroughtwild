# Leyline surface fractures — 6 September 2026

Presentation slice of the [approved reference-led graphics pass](../prototype/leyline-resource-visual-2026-09-06.md). The earlier short pale strips have been replaced with dark, locally branching surface fractures containing thin interrupted cool-white light. This changes neither the native network nor terrain geometry, collision, pressure, gathering or save state.

`CataclysmSites` retains its existing trace tiles and refresh hooks. `LeylineFissures` builds one opaque mesh per tile with one shared material: a dark mouth, shallow chipped mineral lips and a narrow inner strand. Short side branches and subordinate forks taper to an end. Native buried/broken intervals generate no fracture mesh. Exact terrain samples and per-facet building overlap checks suppress unsupported or covered pieces during streaming, excavation, construction and restore.

The active visual settings now live in `game/art/leyline_look.gd` and its resource. The unused old `trace_*`, channel/inlay and regional strip-colour exports were removed from `cataclysm_look.gd` after checking all consumers. Their historical appearance is preserved by the matched baseline captures under `build/leyline-visual/baseline`; historical world-generation inputs remain unchanged.

The first rendered comparison improved depth but made the wide, parallel lips look like a manufactured conduit. The subsequent refinement narrows the main opening to 0.1–0.3 m, varies both chipped edges independently, lowers the maximum lip relief to 1.8 cm and offsets a fine inner strand about 3.5 percent of the nominal mouth width. Side cracks are hairline branches at 28 percent of the main width; most remain dark, with only occasional subdued internal light. Actual exposed-run ends taper; render-tile boundaries no longer create artificial necks.

Other presentation bounds remain 0.4 m conform sampling, 0.9–2.1 m primary branches, eight primary branches per tile, short 0.6 m subsidiary forks and 140 m visibility. Shader variation is slight and slow (47 seconds), with no travelling pulse, point light, transparent overlay or per-frame mesh rebuild. Subpixel mineral flecks fade to their average to limit movement aliasing. Each exported setting documents its visual purpose.

The final integrated rendered review passes 177 checks, including the isolated support probe's 14 checks for missing ground, a three-metre face, complete support loss and exact restoration. Matched V5 seed-1 daylight/dusk images show the refined widths and joins. The sampled tile contains 826 triangles; the generated network totals 42,728 triangles, subject to existing distance culling. Surface lift is 1.8–3.6 cm. The [integrated report](leyline-resource-review-2026-09-06.md) records costs and compatibility checks. Human visual acceptance remains separate; this does not fix the existing cold streaming stalls.
