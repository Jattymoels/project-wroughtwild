# ART-07E2 actual model evidence

The v04 images show the selected editable Blender forge and its six exported
detail candidates in the frozen current native game. The basic and improved
appearances share their plinth and cowl. These are technical source candidates;
owner visual acceptance and normal-game adoption remain pending.

`v04/blender-contact-sheet.jpg` contains 24 actual packed-master reopen renders:
front, back, side, three-quarter, top and underside, both materials and clay.
The individual PNG selections are unchanged renders. `v04/evidence.json` records
all source paths and SHA-256 hashes.

Both renderer prefixes have cold basic/improved, paid firing, actual feeder
controls, paused, completed, shaded and fresh-process paused stills. The two
`work-pause.webp` files contain 60 actual captured frames, resized to 1200×675
and played slowly at 100 ms/frame. Frames 0–47 advance native paid work at 24 Hz;
48–59 retain explicit pause. There are no interpolated animation frames.

The paired JSON reports contain exact native ledgers and check results. Timing
reports come from separate runs with no image generation or capture. These are
application frame intervals on an RTX 5090, not GPU timers or lower-spec claims.

Remaining visible weaknesses include regular stone courses, repeated iron
finish, a narrow workface, bright chamber seams and different renderer exposure.
The existing decorative hearth light is preserved and does not indicate fuel.
The simple review floor and existing feeder are native contract fixtures.
