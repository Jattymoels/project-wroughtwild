# Concept delivery review — 7 September 2026

**Reviewed by Codex. Owner design selection remains pending.**

Four final concept candidates are saved beside the modelling brief: Rimejaw v01,
Kilnback v02, Vaultcrown v01 and Lanternveil v01. The built-in image tool produced
1536 × 1024 PNGs. Each has an overall view, additional angles, material/attachment
details, an ordinary ancestor and proposed scale. They are generated illustrations,
not renders of a consistent three-dimensional model.

| Lanternveil v01 | Dorsal, folded and underside views show wing venation, ordinary moth ancestry and a physically contained abdominal chamber | Some legs/wing roots overlap, so the painting is not proof of six correctly attached legs. Explicitly author three thoracic leg pairs and two wing pairs; resolve folded-wing intersection and emission/opacity in-engine |

Kilnback v01 cropped the left hero tusk and exaggerated the comparison silhouette.
The saved v02 edit corrected the clipping and reduced the silhouette, but did not
fully honour the requested comparison ratio. V01 remains as a superseded review
draft. No further image edit is needed to explore the design; the documented scale
study is authoritative for a later blockout. None of the sheets is certified as a
dimensionally exact turnaround or anatomical blueprint.

The light neutral backgrounds intentionally reveal joints and materials. The
grimdark/fantasy direction is carried by the altered anatomy, aged surfaces and
restrained energy, while the saved owner landscapes retain the environment mood.
No concept introduces a mandatory new hostile role or promises a drop/ability.
