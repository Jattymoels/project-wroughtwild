Use case: stylized-concept. Original Wroughtwild environment production concept board. A carefully composed 3-column by 2-row board with six equally legible scenic habitat vignettes, thin neutral gutters, no text, no lettering, no logos. Every tile shows matching earthy painterly material realism, not cartoons. Top left: gentle meadow woodland edge, broad spreading oak-like tree, thick mixed grass clumps, ferns, brambles and moss, an open footpath and deer at plausible scale. Top middle: Rootvault Wildwood, huge buttressed resinheart trunks, layered pine and broadleaf crowns, resin amber inside a few deeply recessed broken root channels, thick corkbark deadfall, mushrooms and shadowed understory. Top right: Lantern Fen, leaning dark bog oaks and knuckled roots, rust-red clay bank, tall reeds, sedge hummocks, shallow dark water, papery naturally luminous lanternheart husks as rare focal finds. Bottom left: Glasswind Uplands and Shellcut Escarpment, pale fossil shellstone shelves paired with dark layered slate, wind-stunted pine, tough tussocks, low silver-green shrubs, lichens, sheltered pockets of greenery, ram-sized readable routes. Bottom middle: Ember Wastes recovering after catastrophe, bleached ash snags, shattered dark basalt, black grit and rusty ground, patches of coarse surviving grass and thorn scrub, one fractured rock whose deeply recessed scar carries warm ember light, sparse live foliage gives rhythm rather than endless bare flat ground. Bottom right: river and ruined frontier edge, terraced rock banks, damp roots, broken ordinary masonry overgrown by fern and ivy, canopy layers and water with open access for a human. Show nature feeling full in different ways without identical tree density everywhere. Foreground details should be clear enough to inspire individual modular assets. Magic appears only inside a few deep open structural wounds; most plants do not glow. No giant arch, floating rocks, glowing mushrooms everywhere, fantasy ice on the blue region, colourful zone gradients, towns or futuristic machinery.
