# ART-06C — deep lifelines and porcupine face

**Isolated art handoff delivered — 9 September 2026.** The owner wants obvious, deep veins that
look like the mechanism keeping these animals alive, with the porcupine face
repaired alongside. This supersedes ART-06B's deliberately sparse, shallow
treatment. The six approved ancestries and grotesque base augmentations remain.

Affected systems: isolated Blender source finishing, UV scar maps, the standalone
Godot art viewer and evidence. D-013/D-030 apply. This is visual anatomy and lore;
it introduces no healing, combat, progression or meteorite classification rule.
No game tuning file, normal save, world or running playtest is part of the work.

Plan: reproduce the shallow surface and coarse face; test a deeper incision on
the retained porcupine source; extend connected body routes across the six mobs;
repair the porcupine muzzle/whiskers and eyes; reopen and inspect actual dark/lit
meshes, verify depth and pulse in an isolated viewer, and package the result.

Acceptance: visibly broader connected channels, measurable physical recession
with light inside the wound, retained original source hashes and animal detail,
no newly collapsed geometry, and a natural porcupine face. Evidence must include
actual model renders and pulse, with uncertainty stated. Old ART-06B recipes and
evidence remain reproducible. Studio coordinates are not gameplay metres.

Rigs, deformation, runtime LODs, later-era growth and game adoption remain open.

## Result and evidence

The [actual model views and pulse](../art/leyline-studies/2026-09-09/roster-art06c/README.md)
show all six finished surface sources and the porcupine face before/after.
The local editable package is `build/roster-art06c/lifeline-handoff/` (149 files,
approximately 709 MB). [Recipes and controls](../../tools/wroughtwild-roster/LIFELINES.md)
retain the selected revision and reproduction commands. Models and builds stay
local; source recipes, checks and curated visual evidence are versioned.

ART-06B's maximum physical incisions were only 0.000903–0.001604 studio units.
Its noisy-normal and tiny-triangle limits prevented meaningful depth. ART-06C
uses a smooth incision in a consistent flank direction, preserving every
incision triangle's orientation after float32 rounding. Same-ray BVH comparisons
measure every exposed route, rather than accepting a nominal displacement knob.
Median model depth is now **0.03088–0.03159 units**; even the least-deep route's
median is 0.02552. Geometry remains visible with emission disabled. Local safety
protection is recorded and does not erase the required depth.

The channels extend through growth roots, torso and selected limb/wing support
regions. Connected surface distances determine pulse travel. They retain the
original texture beneath darker margins, with energy confined inside the wound.
Both sides are authored and inspected independently. A tortoise route that
jumped through a cavity was rejected and moved onto actual supporting shell.
Fur, folds and overlapping plates still conceal some channel sections.

The porcupine's rigid whisker fan was removed and its roots blended. Early
repairs with protruding stumps, flattened patches and overlapping muzzle UVs
were rejected. The final muzzle has its own unwrapped texture strip, sampled
from the clean original surface. Every original albedo/ORM pixel is retained
above that strip, and both original PNG files remain byte-identical separately.
Ten tapered whiskers have ray-fitted roots; two small non-emissive eyes fit the
measured sockets. The blunt muzzle, ears and source animal identity remain.

Porcupine cleanup includes coincident UV-vertex welding, local cap/degeneracy
repair and smoothing. Its host has 280,126 triangles, with 4,432 separately
recorded face-attachment triangles. Other source counts remain unchanged except
the tortoise's previously documented zero-area face (295,369 remain). All six
raw GLB hashes are unchanged. No new asset generation or dependency was needed.

## Verification and limits

- **72 Blender reopening checks:** exact host/attachment geometry, source
  identity, packed maps, retained UVs and off/lit views, plus face close-ups.
- **84 checks in each Godot renderer:** all 59 previous surface checks and 25
  additional attachment checks. Actual creature pixels change under light and
  travel; pause/resume, instance phase and colour isolation remain correct.
- **95 handoff checks:** each route's measured depth, original source and image
  bytes, preserved porcupine atlas pixels, broader contained cores, both rendered
  checks and 48 actual frames of a four-second pulse.
- **12 regression checks:** the shared builder reproduces ART-06B's selected
  porcupine geometry, albedo, ORM and scar map exactly.

The packaged source import and file manifest are checked separately. Only the
known Windows root-certificate-store startup diagnostic remains; no script,
shader or Blender assertion error is accepted in the final runs.

The review uses two-unit studio normalisation, 0.032 maximum incision and
0.078/0.063/0.026 channel/damage/core half-widths. The four-second pulse has 32%
residual light, 2.4 peak and a 0.20 crest. Its timing-join radius is 0.045 units.
The [recipe](../../tools/wroughtwild-roster/LIFELINES.md) explains these and the
porcupine's source-specific envelope, smoothing, whisker and atlas controls.
None is gameplay size, power or sustain tuning.

This is a visual finishing checkpoint, not full ART-06 completion. Dense source
fur, folds and small muzzle follicle irregularities remain visible in close-up.
Full facial articulation, body rigs, deformation, runtime detail levels and
populated-world performance remain unverified. Later-era physical additions,
boss art and native adoption stay queued. Final human visual acceptance of
this revised treatment is separate from the completed technical checks.
