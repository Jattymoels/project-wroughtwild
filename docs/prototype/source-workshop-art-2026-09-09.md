## Outcome and existing contract

Make one Red salt inclusion, its released Red Salt and one Red Heat Buffer
visually related: dark broken mineral/ceramic host, recessed damage, narrow
ember core. Produce an editable Blender/runtime handoff and an isolated Godot
review showing actual native ownership and work states. Preserve the existing
1.5 × 1.1 × 1.5 m source and 1 × 1.2 × 1 m buffer bodies and working ports.

The selected [LF-2C rules](living-frontier-wave2-2026-09-08.md) remain authoritative:
four deliberate extraction steps release a 16-unit lot; the source owns its
claim until capacity-checked collection. Two Red Salt charge one stored heat
(maximum four). One brick firing reserves eight clay, one mechanical stroke
and one heat, then produces four bricks. Red heat does not replace winding.
Blocked work pauses without spending twice. Cancellation returns exact escrow.
Exhaustion/reformation follows the existing source clock, including obstruction.

Affected presentation: source host, released pieces, buffer fitting, scar
material and isolated review. D-010, D-013/D-030, D-017, D-031–033 and existing
source/machine tuning apply. No new recipes, resources, costs, physics envelopes,
geography, progression, save schema or runtime art adoption are selected.

4. Check material/body fit, ownership edge cases, pause/restart and asset reopen;

5. Update the queue, commit the checked scoped handoff and push normally.

Released pieces remain a visual representation of a source-owned claim. They
