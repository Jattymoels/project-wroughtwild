## Delivered result

- One original built-in ImageGen source and local TRELLIS v0.6.0 export, retained
  unchanged with prompt, seed 42, runtime/model revision and exact hashes. The
  282,324-triangle source keeps its inclined pale mineral planes, weathering and
  existing texture charts. Corrected face orientation and attached dark incision,
  core/travel masks and tangent normals keep the same surface readable with the
  light off. A full voxel repair/rebake was rejected after rendered comparison:
  it made the stone soft and distorted its appearance.
- Three closed, irregular fragments cut at actual scar-bearing surface locations.
  Initial cage baking missed cut faces and produced black patches; subdividing
  and reducing the convex chips also created slivers that opened after export.
  The finished chips retain their closed hulls. Their independent 1k atlases
  project each texel onto one actual source triangle, using barycentric UVs from
  that triangle's chart. No interpolation crosses unrelated source charts. Their
  normal maps are baked in the fragments' own tangent frames. All retain a core
  mask and pass the original closed-volume/export checks.
- One construction-sized White Connection: broad timber foot, braced mast,
  forged collars and compression jaws, a captured portion of the same mineral,
  and a terminal reaching the existing 1.18 m signal endpoint. All housing and
  mineral remain inside the unchanged 0.65 × 1.18 × 0.55 m body at every LOD.
- One isolated Godot review driven by real native state. Natural readiness has
  a four-second gathering swell and short passage. A native White counter change
  gives the post a 0.85-second one-shot pulse. An unwound drum may receive that
  request without moving; a blocked/disconnected signal has no passage. Restoring
  historical counters never replays them. Review pause freezes the pulse phase.
  Cargo movement, winding, collection and exhaustion retain their native owners.

The review preserves the paid checkpoint's lever/post/drum/landing relative

The source's non-manifold junctions limit clean reduction. The initial 2.5k far
target was not achieved; three conservative detail levels preserve its shape.
A substantially cheaper distant mesh needs a later topology pass. The source
remains an exterior mesh, not a certified geological solid; only the fragments
have the closed-volume guarantee. Newly exposed chip faces are projected source
appearance, not an independently observed mineral interior.

At 1600 × 1000 on the RTX 5090, the isolated Forward+ scene measured median GPU
render times of 0.35 ms for the source view, 0.37 ms for the post and 0.55 ms for
the complete route; p95 was 0.35–0.56 ms across six steady day/shade cases. CPU
render medians were 0.107–0.148 ms, with 80–133 draw calls. Resident textures were
approximately **537 MiB**, including all loaded detail levels, repeated fallback
maps and reused grove context. This is not normal-world FPS or a production
texture budget. Lower-spec tests, texture cooking, first-person placement/support,
streaming and populated-world adoption remain ART-05 work.
