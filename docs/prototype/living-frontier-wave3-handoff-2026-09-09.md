The current user request and Wave 2 review require proof before replacement,
while complete laboratory acquisition belongs to later encounters. Wave 3 adds
useful host materials and proves manufacture; it keeps the coherent current
Forge/curio campaign. Before selecting a successor policy, review all 15 owned
offensive IDs and these independent paths together:

| Existing path | Wave 3 treatment / later requirement |
| --- | --- |

## Limits and next review boundary

Travel, resource dispatch and formation time are accelerated in the paid fixture.
Its actual costs and save boundaries are evidence; novice navigation, enjoyable
cadence and final combat difficulty still need a human session. The three-class
combat fixture uses explicit ordinary-ingredient setup; the separate paid Warden
run supplies acquisition evidence. Generation was sampled across 37 deterministic
seeds, not every accepted integer seed.

Existing articulated skins/scar masks and simple local geometry are placeholders.
These images are not acceptance of final animal or laboratory art. Concurrent
TRELLIS boar/grove production, normal user saves and unrelated files were not
adopted or used as fixtures. The current scar adapter follows actual attack states;
the engine controller owns habitat travel. Source and machine domain ownership
remain native. The source payload retains its published format tag under the new
outer world identity; no legacy payload rewrite is required.

This work stops after Wave 3 for orchestrator review. Laboratory encounters,
hybrids, failsafes, physical era transitions, the human boss and configurable Heat
were not started. Future transformation safety must still prove occupied/support
space, doors, workstation clearance, excavation, stores/drops, attached sources
and an ordinary approach through physical change and pending-event restart.
Current paid-building tests establish that those regions remain usable now;
they do not claim that an unimplemented transformation is already safe.
