# Vertical Slice

**Status:** Proposed prototype target  
**Goal:** Prove one complete world → base → trial → base capability loop.

## Player journey

The player begins in a small valley with crude equipment and a basic shelter location. A visible trial is initially difficult because its boss deals substantial fire damage.

1. Gather wood and iron.
2. Construct a shelter, storage and basic forge.
3. Produce useful iron fittings for a mine reinforcement order.
4. Increase Blacksmithing from level 1 to the required level.
5. Use payment and recovered materials to upgrade the forge.
6. Craft an ordinary fire-resistant armour piece.
7. Enter the repeatable trial with one persistent class build.
8. Choose temporary boons through branching rooms.
9. Recover an Ember Catalyst and either defeat or learn from the boss.
10. Use the catalyst during tempering to improve the armour.
11. Re-enter and defeat the boss more reliably.
12. Unlock one new construction material or capability as the visible completion reward.

## Included systems

- one bounded authored or seed-generated valley;
- wood and iron resource gathering;
- six to eight construction shapes;
- storage by material family;
- one forge and one forge upgrade;
- Blacksmithing levels 1–5;
- one useful bulk order;
- one starting class and two or three skills;
- a small equipment property pool;
- one short trial with branching choices;
- approximately 12–20 boons and three weaknesses;
- three enemy behaviours and one boss;
- one crafting catalyst;
- trial and open-world death contracts;
- save and reload of player, build and placed structures.

## Explicit exclusions

Owner-approved D-030 adds the bounded [cataclysm art/generation intensive](cataclysm-world-intensive-2026-09-06.md)
to the later prototype: composed impact/leyline/ruin relationships and local
authored asset integration. It preserves finite world scope and existing gameplay
contracts. No external production-art service or automation network is required.

The original exclusions below describe the first slice. Owner-approved
D-027/D-028 bring forward only the eight-family resource-habitat expansion and
one Forge endgame loop described in the [world](world-intensive-2026-09-06.md)
and [trial](trial-intensive-2026-09-06.md) work items. Economy trading, map items,
new eras and broad production remain excluded.

- multiplayer, co-op and raids;
- infinite terrain or destructive voxel terrain;
- multiple finished classes;
- class-hall content beyond one representative upgrade path;
- broad factory logistics or unattended resource generation;
- trains, portals, flight or multiple major outposts;
- complex structural integrity;
- endgame maps, economies or trading markets;
- production art and audio.

## Prototype tuning questions

1. Does the player understand why the trial is currently difficult?
2. Does blacksmithing repetition feel useful because the mine consumes the output?
3. Does the forge upgrade feel like control rather than a passive stat increase?
4. Does the persistent build remain recognisable after temporary boons are added?
5. Does the catalyst drop create an exciting and understandable crafting decision?
6. Does dying encourage another attempt rather than abandonment?
7. Does the final capability make the world or building possibilities feel meaningfully larger?

## Completion condition

The slice is successful when a new player can complete the loop, explain how each system helped another system, and express a desire to pursue a newly visible ambition without being told what the hypothetical full game contains.

## Bounded pressure workshop — D-031

The owner-approved [pressure workshop](pressure-workshop-2026-09-06.md) adds one
finite source in a V5 asteroid-struck pre-cataclysm smithy, one kit and one
existing brick recipe. This narrowly expands the production boundary: no
offline work, renewable supply or broad factory. The player's construction
contains accidental pressure; the ruin does not imply civilisation after impact.
Current visual finish is accepted for now, with a later graphical pass deferred.
