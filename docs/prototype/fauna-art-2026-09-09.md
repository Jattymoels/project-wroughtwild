## Delivered result

See the [actual engine evidence and full report](../art/leyline-studies/2026-09-09/fauna-art03/README.md)
and [rebuild recipe](../../tools/wroughtwild-fauna/README.md). The wolf now has
a separated articulated lower muzzle, simple fitted mouth interior, shallow
surface-fitted eyes, recessed scars and seven clips on a 17-bone fitted rig.
One locally generated stag has an open rigid crown, fitted eyes, neck/shoulder
scars and four clips on a 17-bone rig. The boar and liked moth are retained in
an isolated copy of the approved grove for day/shade/dusk comparison.

Both supply packed editable Blender sources, three measured detail levels,
base/ORM/normal/fracture maps, pause-aware Godot materials, actual movement loops
and import/inspection checks. Local package: `build/fauna-art03/fauna-handoff/`.
The exact new stag input, prompt, generation settings and untouched source
hash are recorded. Existing wolf, moth and boar source hashes remain unchanged.
Fresh copies of both packaged projects passed reimport and engine checks;
both packed Blender deliverables also passed reopening/deformation checks.
The linked result includes the clean local package's file/hash manifest.

Validation: 148 wolf and 85 stag GLB/map/source checks passed; reopened Blender
sources passed 77 / 36 deformed-pose samples, respectively. All three detail
levels passed Godot rig, clip, skin, feet, material-phase and pause checks.
Actual skin spikes initially missed by structural tests were reproduced and
fixed with anatomical limb masks and connected weight diffusion; the 80 mm
edge-extension rejection and 1 mm floor tolerance were not relaxed. The full
result records maximum measured errors and the limits of these sampled checks.

Rendering measurements cover one/24 actors, three detail levels, static versus
updated animation and scar off/on. At mid detail, 24 animated wolves measured
0.408 ms median GPU time; 24 stags 0.445 ms on this RTX 5090, Forward+, 1280×960.
These isolated measurements do not establish full-game or minimum-spec costs.

## Controls and remaining limits

Presentation controls live in `tools/wroughtwild-fauna/wolf.json`, `stag.json`
and the matching `*-rig.json`, with purpose descriptions. Both use a four-second
pulse, 22% minimum and 2.8 peak; core/damage half-widths are 5/18 mm for the wolf
and 6/25 mm for the stag. These are art controls, not progression or combat tuning.
Source-specific skin smoothing is 18 passes for the wolf and 80 for the stag,
preserving rigid skull/crown and planted sole anchors.

The final stag `graze` clip is low-plant browsing. A deeper grass-level bend was
visually rejected because its generated neck plates folded poorly, even after
numeric checks passed. Deeper grazing needs further neck/plate work. Generated
triangle topology, simple mouth internals, rigid moss strips, some raised wolf
mineral roots and uncompressed/repeated fallback textures remain limitations.
Native Enemy adapters, terrain IK, actual travelling locomotion, body/collider
fitting and populated-world performance are not claimed by this art slice.
