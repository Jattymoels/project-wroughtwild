# Leyline extraction — useful media, rare finds and perfected Catalysts

**Status: Design proposal; acquisition rules unchanged.**
Owner clarification: 8 September 2026. Baseline: `49edab9`.
Companion to the [Living Frontier roadmap](living-frontier-roadmap-2026-09-08.md).

## 1. The clarified contract

The owner specifies a primary magic resource **other than a Catalyst**, useful
for crafting, refining and building. An intact Catalyst can rarely be found
during extraction; eventually the player perfects a process to manufacture one.
Catalysts should remain rare or difficult because their effects are distinctive.

This replaces the earlier two-Faint-Catalysts introductory-haul assumption and
the proposed prohibition on new manufacture. **Vein material supplies ordinary
magical craft and deliberate Catalyst manufacture; intact Catalysts are rare
finds.** The latter route must not be the only way through the campaign.

Recommend initially applying this to the five offensive Catalyst identities.
Defence/life/speed Kinds remain existing encounter rewards unless the owner
selects a broader change. Their lore may still come from augmented hosts.
This document proposes replacements; it does not classify current accepted
acquisition as a bug or confiscate already owned items.

## 2. A useful primary material

Recommend one harvested medium per influence, used directly in a practical
process and eventually Catalyst manufacture. Four materials now have an
explicit purpose from the user's brief. They are material inputs, not new
trade currencies, quality ranks or an extra talent system.

| Influence | Candidate medium/property | First consumer to define |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | Strain-bearing mineral; bounded stored mechanical work | One handling/pressure-work operation with an exact debit |
| Red | Heat-bearing salts/inclusions; controlled heat delivery | One fired-material recipe or furnace process with a useful building result |
| Blue | Binding flakes; controlled material/process retention | One setting/refining operation or retaining fixture |
| Green | Pattern-bearing resin; repeated binding/growth | One useful wood/fibre assembly or bounded transfer process |

Names, recipe amounts and effects are unselected. For the first Red proof,
choose either an additive to an existing firing process or one selected
material treatment. Specify what inputs it replaces/supplements, what changes
and why the result is useful. No current recipe already accepts these proposed
media. Cinderglass/windows provide a possible destination, but a cosmetic
window alone does not prove a useful new magical processing property.

Avoid a compulsory medium → powder → essence → refined essence ladder.
Local capture/processing state can stay in a device transaction. An intermediate
item needs its own useful consumer or transport/storage decision.

## 3. Read, expose, settle, draw, collect

1. A ready source has a seeded host, actual material stock, discoverable route
   and clear workplace. Introductory access precedes a successful Trial,
   improved forge, Catalyst or rare device.
2. Inspection identifies ordinary output and work. Details can explain a rare
   intact pattern without promising this node contains one.
3. Ordinary contextual work exposes casing/layers/branches with visible change.
   Progress survives disengagement, streaming and restart.
4. Settle the exposed flow and draw a **fixed source lot**. The reliable default
   action completes it. Optional timing/property shortcuts can reduce work;
   missed inputs do not destroy a rare result or invent unavoidable damage.
5. Release guaranteed ordinary material and any rare intact result into exact
   owned claims. Collection uses the correct material/Kind path once; full
   inventory retains the output with its existing owner.
6. Pay the ordinary station/recipe costs for a practical result. Verify a whole
   expedition with **zero Catalysts** still provides something worth making.

Manual work needs no Catalyst, specific elemental attack, late blueprint or
rare core. This follows D-021: baseline work works; compatible properties can
buy time/yield through an explicit host response. Do not require a new spawned
defence wave at every source; existing habitat danger can matter.

## 4. One fair rare opportunity per source lot

Define an indivisible extraction lot independent of the UI batch and input
count. Roll at most once per lot. Splitting work into clicks, changing tools,
cancelling, taking single units or reopening a panel cannot create extra rolls.

Use stable source-anchor, manifestation and lot identities. Resolve the hidden
result deterministically or persist it before any repeatable work/claim operation
could reroll it. Commit ordinary and rare outputs together at completion, with
separate inventory claims if needed. Sound, glow and particles cannot grant
another output.

Rarity describes frequency, not potency. Initial intact finds can use existing
Faint identities; Stable/Potent retain D-026 refinement. Greater bulk yield from
a tool or building demand must not accidentally multiply rare opportunities
per unit. Choose richer-source lot counts or rare rates explicitly and show
their actual effect in Details.

Measure droughts as well as averages. For illustration only, a 5% chance leaves
about 36% of players without a find after 20 independent opportunities. This is
not a recommended rate; it shows why rare randomness cannot be the sole
progression route. Useful guaranteed material and later luck-independent
manufacture provide alternatives. No pity-counter system is required initially.

## 5. Perfecting Catalyst manufacture

| Stage | Capability | Meaningful requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw work | Use the medium in a practical process | Ordinary source, station and inputs |
| Controlled work | Improve one process through reusable equipment | Useful completed work and a concrete investment |
| Learn the pattern | Understand a lab method or useful craft milestone | An earned lesson reachable without a Catalyst |
| Perfect the process | Dependably manufacture a selected Catalyst | Substantial matching medium, ordinary process inputs, known method, facility and craft competence |
| Refine quality | Upgrade Faint → Stable → Potent | Existing same-identity D-026 material/skill/facility/era costs |

Recommend deterministic manufacture once requirements are met. Difficulty comes
from learning, preparation and expenditure, rather than an expensive random
failure destroying the whole investment. Raw material remains useful before
mastery; unsuccessful optional investigation must not become compulsory junk
production.

The perfected recipe takes **raw medium**, not an input Catalyst. It therefore
works for an unlucky player. Its lesson/facility cannot require a lucky Catalyst
or a boss balanced around having one. Earn competence through useful work under
the existing craft-skill discipline, not a separate valve-turning XP bar.

Select a substantial material cost alongside ordinary craft/build demand, so
manufacture is a deliberate choice. Do not let a trivial recipe flood early
play with transformative items. No numeric cost or exact lesson gate is selected
here; measure the journey before fixing one.

## 6. Four media, five existing offensive identities

The current identities are Ember, Frost, Preserving, Piercing and Impact, each
with three grades. Four colours do not require a one-to-one mapping or justify
making an existing identity unobtainable.

| Candidate source/process | Existing result | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red heat pattern | Ember | Directly matches the owner's Cinder moth example |
| Blue binding pattern | Frost | Preserve actual Frost/control mechanics |
| White compression | Impact | A contact/release identity rather than merely more damage |
| White channel/through-flow | Piercing | Same medium with a distinct learned process or host form |
| Green pattern carried onward | Preserving | Proposed fit with held-effect/newcomer/return readings; inspect those mechanics before selecting |

Blue could also support Preserving. Green might later justify a new Propagating
identity, but that requires its own Foundry/crafting compatibility design. Choose
the map in INT-10 rather than forcing lore around a name or silently adding a
sixth family. An intact host uses an authored eligible pool; perfected recipes
choose a known result. Mixing media need not create new currencies when existing
Kinds and ordered Foundry interactions already express combinations.

## 7. Recurring nodes and sustainable raw supply

Recommend a **finite seeded network, fixed eligible hosts, finite individual
lots and bounded recurring magical material**. This is a proposed renewability
exception for the selected media. It supports consumed magical craft and later
Catalyst manufacture in a finite world.

A permanent anchor persists after extraction. Each manifestation owns finite
lots, rare results and partial work. Later saved formation creates a new one
subject to catchment budget and valid space. Rendering, scene arrival and
restarting a process cannot initialise stock.

Initial recommendation: ready first sources; capped formation during active
overworld play including distant catchments; no offline, paused or Trial-time
growth; caps on ready stock **and accumulated formation credit**. Reward useful
circuits of exploring/fighting/building, not standing beside a timer. Numeric
cadence follows testing. Use visible forming/ready/spent stages with concise cues.

Keep ordinary deposits, timber, the five rare components' field sources and
D-031 pressure finite. Preserve the existing D-029 repeatable Forge boss
replenishment of rare fixture components. Era transformations may introduce explicitly new hosts/opportunities
but cannot reroll partly worked lots, reset depletion or multiply already formed
stock. New-era yield/odds changes apply to future declared formations only.

Later lab sources can supply finite material lots per eligible cleared room/run
through visible connected apparatus, under an explicit repeatable-supply rule.
Saved run/offer identity owns the batch; reloading, suspending or entering/exiting
cannot regenerate it. This supplements field supply instead of making the
capstone compulsory for sustainable crafting.

## 8. Tools and outposts after manual work proves itself

A reusable brace can reduce repeated preparation; a survey aid improves local
direction/type reading; a collector supplies clear work controls and a small
output holder. Choose one benefit and compare it with manual work before adding
more. A scanner does not spawn nodes or become mandatory to see them.

An outpost earns its place through source access, ordinary resources, processing,
storage, shelter and a useful route. It remains valuable between formations and
after resonance. Approved fixture attachment positions and work circles must
survive building/excavation; occupying the only node is not mandatory.

Assistance does not grant unattended mastery, duplicate rare rolls or remote
access to chests/death packs. Natural formation while distant is not permission
for machines to run offscreen. Signals request work; energy and inputs pay.
Efficiency initially changes effort/handling rather than secretly raising rare
chance. A later precision process may change odds only through an explicit
displayed paid rule. Existing rare cores keep their distinct jobs.

## 9. Replace acquisition coherently

The desired origin is now vein material/intact patterns plus manufacture from
that material. Recommend routine wild-mob Catalyst payouts move out of the new
economy so extraction matters; retain other Kinds, gear and useful host materials.
A rare cache containing a previously manufactured Catalyst would be an explicit
possible exception, not assumed here.

The audit covers 15 owned offensive IDs: five canonical identities and their
Stable/Potent variants. Treat these source paths together when the successor
policy is selected:

| Current path | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary family/additional offensive Catalyst rolls | Replace in successor worlds with selected retained rewards/useful material and measure satisfaction |
| Guaranteed elite family-Kind award | Prevent unintended offensive creation; explicitly select its retained reward |
| Four elite-prefix bounties | Audit their independent Ember/Preserving grants, beyond the family table |
| Legacy/story/repeatable `catalyst` room | Adapt to a real source/processing opportunity and chosen protection contract; no disguised corpse/button grant |
| `distil_ember` from iron/charcoal alone | Retire/rework only under the new policy after raw usefulness and later manufacture exist; current behaviour is accepted D-026 |
| Peddler exchange into Frost/Preserving/Piercing/Impact | Remove or explicitly restrict the bypass; Ember is already outside that exchange set |
| Same-identity Faint → Stable → Potent | Preserve unless deliberately revised; this transforms owned stock with current costs |
| Lifting/refunds/deposits/saved drops/earned rewards | Preserve exact ownership; these are transfers rather than new source creation |

Bosses do not universally drop Ember: the guaranteed current grant is the
designated Catalyst room. Completion separately pays gear, curios and repeatable
components. Preserve meaningful Trial rewards and the non-offensive Kinds where
actually granted. Deleting drops alone is not a completed economy pass.

Do not remove the existing low-level fallback globally in a partial prototype.
Test the no-Catalyst opening and luck-independent manufacture path before the
new acquisition becomes the ordinary successor experience.

## 10. Death, saves and exact claims

Current Faint offensive Catalysts behave as inventory materials; Stable/Potent
are purse currencies. Current Trial death protection checks the exact Faint
Ember reward ID. An intact Frost find would not inherit it automatically.
New output ownership needs a visible selected contract.

Recommendation to review: raw Trial media follows existing at-risk material
rules; successfully **secured intact Catalysts** receive consistent protection
across selected offensive identities. This explicitly broadens the Ember
exception and requires D-006/Trial selection. Undrawn stock and unfinished work
are not earned rewards. Open-world outputs initially use their actual category
and show that consequence; a uniform all-grade death policy is separate.

The sim owns host/manifestation/lot IDs, material stock, hidden rare outcome,
work, clocks, recipes and claims. Godot presents them and handles input/physical
collection through native transactions. Release creates one output owner;
collection transfers it once. Full storage retains the claim.

Later devices reserve inputs/capacity before work. Cancellation returns escrow
once; dismantling returns contents and selected reusable parts without refilling
the source. Pending rare outcomes survive both. Restore a whole checkpoint
rather than merging source/device/inventory records from different saves.

Recommend successor world/economy/campaign versioning. V1–V6 retain geography,
acquisition and original campaign until separately migrated. Preserve items,
grades, Foundry placements, equipment, loose loot and suspended rewards. The
resonance transforms in the roadmap must carry source/work records with the
physical changes. Unknown versions refuse or follow explicit migration; they
never default to a new full source.

## 11. Tuning and proof

| Control | Player purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Raw lot/haul | A zero-Catalyst expedition funds a useful operation |
| Work effort | Tangible extraction without delaying all expression behind chores |
| Rare chance per canonical lot / pool | Frequency and identity of intact patterns, independent of input spam |
| Formation cadence / stock / credit caps | Useful revisit circuits versus camping and unlimited hoarding |
| Direct-use recipes | Material has value before manufacture |
| Perfected process cost / lesson / facility | A chosen Catalyst is hard-earned but luck-independent |
| Tool/process benefit | Effort, throughput, quality and rare chance remain explicit separate effects |
| Era source changes | More extreme places provide new opportunities without resetting old work |
| Heat reward axes | Harder runs improve declared opportunities, not automatic potency |

No production numbers are selected. Test raw-only, unlucky and lucky journeys;
zero-found-Catalyst manufacture; legitimate grade refining/lifting; exact lot
payouts; cancel/reload exploits; distant formation/blocked hosts; source state
through resonance; old worlds; Trial securing; and repeated postgame consumption.

Measure time to useful raw work, distribution to first rare interaction, cost
of manufacture versus building, and the travel/combat/workshop mix. Accounting
tests cannot establish rarity feels exciting or that a trip is worthwhile.
