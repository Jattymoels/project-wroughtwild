## Remaining visual and performance limits

- **C5:** raised ore fits the existing 2.6 × 0.6 × 0.9 m fallback body. Normal
  faceted terrain instead uses a thin surface ribbon, which the adapter keeps.
  These raised slabs are not cleared to replace ordinary terrain ore or its
  excavation behavior. Repetitive slabs, soft mineral marks, silver highlights
  and small open mesh boundaries remain. LOD targets pass, but meshes are not
  claimed watertight. This placement boundary is explicit in the G1 prompt.
- **C6:** original bodies, transforms and geography are retained. Masonry remains
  regular, roots angular/thin, scar triangulation visible and native foreground
  clutter can obscure the authored forms. The source kit alone does not produce
  the density of the selected world concept. Fixed-view RTX results do not
  establish a wider hardware or density budget.
- **F2:** the source is softened and depth-compressed to fit the native envelope;
  coil repetition and native winding-angle snapping remain. There is no new
  persistent stump or hand animation. LOD selection is explicit, not automatic.
- **F3:** simple native source mounts/weathering remain. Ventlung far deliberately
  retains its **8,500-triangle middle mesh** because the 3,750-triangle candidate
  lost the folds. The combined estimated RGBA8 texture footprint with mipmaps is
  **127,227,217 bytes**, including retained material inputs. This is a measured
  cost to review, not a cleared production budget. Ordinary-world automatic LOD
  and full rollout remain future work.

Earlier reports retain other source silhouette, topology and missed-budget
limits. No asset cost or gameplay tuning was silently changed by publication.
