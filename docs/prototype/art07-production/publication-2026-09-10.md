## Visual and performance limits

- **B1:** full-width source crowns preserve the tree language and attached
  foliage, but are open-grown candidates, not a finished dense forest. Near
  oak/pine meshes are approximately 219k/395k triangles; far meshes remain
  65–70k. LODs and duplicate textures stay resident, and hard transitions remain.
  The native work fixture narrows the whole tree to fit its existing collider;
  that distorted fit is not cleared for normal-world use. Full-width collision,
  sloped root seating, dense composition and practical forest budgets remain
  open. B4 must assess these limits before wider adoption.
- **D1:** geometry and native fit are delivered; provisional grain and repeated
  board divisions are visibly simpler than the concept cottage. Final materials
  and later roof/fine-piece slices remain separate. The ten meshes total 3,408
  triangles, with one surface each.
- **D4:** the seven material identities are distinct, but patterns are more
  regular than the hand-worked concept. Large walls need appropriate per-board
  UV variation. Surface maps do not provide physical frames, pegs or joinery.
  The source texture estimate is 140 MiB with conservative RGBA mip chains;
  the review also carries imported duplicates and render targets.
- **F5:** the approved originals are retained, including their approximately
  3 mm incisions and relatively dense White far mesh. Whole-review texture
  allocations were roughly 408–770 MiB. No later creature-style scar redesign
  or texture-budget clearance is implied.

Actual Blender and both-renderer evidence was inspected, rather than treating
concept images as completed models. Worker timings come from small RTX 5090
reviews; they do not establish full-world or lower-spec performance. Visual
acceptance of these delivered packages remains open; prior approval of the
retained ART-04 designs is preserved.
