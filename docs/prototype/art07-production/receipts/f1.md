## Implementation and review

The heart is a folded seed/lantern organ in a thick five-petal husk. The same
intact core appears in the physical pickup and the lamp's direct timber/reed
cage. Stormglass is a rough open-ended mineral tube retained by two fitted iron
rings and small seats, beneath the existing lever/tapping motion. Its direct
source bed has a real open split and remains after finite stock is exhausted.
The neutral source housings are independent of native stock and grant nothing.

Original raw meshes remain in a hidden source collection. Separate finished,
runtime, mechanism, source-host and review collections are packed into the
editable master. Surface recovery uses source-triangle interpolation of UVs and
material indices, then measured geometric incisions. The tube receives an open
20 mm radius bore with a cylindrical chart; the final recovery passes an axial
clearance ray. Every core LOD is closed and has no welded boundary/nonmanifold
edges or degenerate triangles. Rings use transported frames and a closed seam.

All ordinary maps use D4/D6 metric UVs. Original organic albedo/ORM remain intact;

samples. Blender emission-off/clay views retain those cuts. Godot excludes mask

existing native warm light remains; channel emission switches off. A lever event

markers are retained and are distinct from the component's channel light.

`settings.json` explains all exposed presentation controls: dimensions, source

1.25/.22/1.5/.6. They change presentation only; native pulse timing and gameplay
