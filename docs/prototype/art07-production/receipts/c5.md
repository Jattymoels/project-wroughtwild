## Implementation and review

- Selected `kit-v08`: v07 repaired-host geometry plus final microbump material
  and packed ORM. Its 90 GLBs are byte-identical to v07. Thirty native stock /
  crack combinations have near/middle/far exports. Every worked face is a real
  cut with separate cap UVs and continuing mineral fields, not an extra prop.
- Iron has dull ochre nodules/streaks; copper has green-brown folded bands in
  warm host; tin has pale pockets in dark stone; ember-iron has a recessed
  internal fracture; silver has a narrow metallic seam. There are no universal
  bright crystal spikes. Native cold specimens do not emit.
- The measured native fallback body is 2.6 × 0.6 × 0.9 m, centred 0.3 m above
  the anchor, with original seeded yaw. Source dimensions are Blender
  2.48 × 0.78 × 0.50 m; export maps `(x,y,z)` to Godot `(x,z,-y)` once. Bottom
  centre pivot; no sockets. Every candidate fits the actual rotated body and
  all five real capsule work approaches remain clear. Models add no collider.
- **Ordinary terrain contact remains an adoption limitation.** Its current ore
  collider is a zero-thickness, approximately 3 × 0.48 m ribbon lifted 0.018 m
  over actual surface support (24 steps, 144 triangles, 432 collider points).
  The C5 adapter preserves that exact original path. Gap removal/restoration is
  checked against the original geometry and collision arrays, with unchanged
  anchors and stock. Raised sources are demonstrated only in the fallback
  fixture. They are not cleared for ordinary terrain placement or excavation.
- Geometric scar target is 0.055 m deep with 0.07 m half-width. Independent
  packed-source measurements: copper/silver 0.0549873 m, tin 0.0549786 m,
  ember 0.0429906 m. The ordinary ember channel exists without heat; the other
  four-ore crack candidates map native crack state, with no invented iron crack.
  Clay and emission-off renders retain the recessed shape.
- Six images remain packed: original 2048/1024 albedo/ORM pairs plus selected
  1024 shared albedo/ORM. Albedo is sRGB and ORM is linear. The new periodic
  stone material avoids original-atlas stretching after reduction. Runtime
  exports share exactly two maps; geometry bufferViews are copied byte-for-byte.
  Mineral/scar/travel data are vertex-colour R/G/B; original/cap UVs are retained.
  Explicit Godot shading approximates the authored Blender material under each
  renderer's lighting. Two-sided opaque surfaces; no alpha, bloom or scar lights.
- Native heat authorises pulse and inherited tint/emission. Clock period is
  3.5 s and pauses with the study's Space handler or native scene-tree pause.
  H is an explicitly posed full-ember preview in the static display. Static
  specimens have no collision and do not claim work/ownership. Native work is
  a separate fixture using actual ResourceNode, player pickup, inventory and
  SaveManager. Heat is applied through native `soak` as a test setup, not a
  paid first-hour furnace route. No ore is granted into inventory.
- Existing finite yields remain 8/8/6/6/6 in catalogue order, two per press,
  no tool. Iron needs no heat; others need native level 2. Era remains 1/2/2/3/3.
  Cold and level-one refusals preserve stock, native cold/heavy impact crack
  heated ore, heat is transient and cracks/stock/geography survive reload.
- Visible limitations: repeated low slab silhouette, softened copper bands and
  tin pocket detail, strong silver highlights. All 90 exports retain small
  boundary defects (maximum 21 edges after a 1 µm weld); none is certified
  watertight. No degenerate or nonfinite triangles remain. The glTF exporter
  emitted mesh-validity warnings; independent imports and actual renders passed.
  These are source candidates for visual review, not owner-approved final art.
- Rejected diagnostics remain local: v01 markings too regular; v02/v03 over
  triangle budgets; v04 degenerate failure; v05 bounds failure; v06 visually
  fractured reduction and stretched atlas despite later technical passes.
  None of those is the delivered runtime. v07/v08 resolve the large surface
  failures; the listed smaller visual/topology limitations remain explicit.
