## Implementation and review

- Separate SOURCE, FINISHED, RUNTIME and REVIEW collections; one closed mesh and
  one material surface per shape. Two-step stairs are a single closed profile;
  both diagonal forms retain the native `x <= z` half-space at zero rotation.
  Fieldstone courses have a closed backing, coarse worn corners and uneven caps.
- [Dimensions, pivots and controls](../../../../tools/wroughtwild-art07/d1/README.md)
  list all ten native sizes and costs. Object origins stay at the native pose;
  low-piece geometry has centre Y -0.25 m and spans -0.5 to 0.0 m. Blender
  `(x,y,z)` exports once to Godot `(x,z,-y)`, with identity object transforms.
- Ordinary pieces remain unlit. Backed 8 mm wide / 6 mm deep joinery grooves
  are physical joints, not magical scars. The required deep-scar treatment does
  not apply to these ordinary materials. No pulse or mechanism was invented.
- Two packed 128 × 256 sRGB provisional colour images support Blender review.
  The copied game's adapter applies the unchanged existing native family/role
  materials. No normal, ORM, emission or new gameplay texture is introduced.
- Selected geometry: `v02/models-v06`. Earlier model-v01 failed the exact
  triangle-extents assertion; models-v02/v03 were inspection candidates.
  V04 preserved acute mating edges, corrected the native diagonal orientation,
  bounded tread cuts to exposed steps and retained irregular fieldstone wear.
  Independent import then exposed a three-edge footing triangulation defect.
  V05 caught it before export; V06 triangulates warped stone faces before boolean
  union, cleans microscopic duplicate junctions and commits explicit triangles.
  All original manifold, degenerate-face and dimension assertions remain.
- Known visual limits: simple provisional grain, coarse faceting and repeated
  board divisions are less rich than the building board. Final material art is
  separate. The plain clipped block retains its simple exact prism. This is a
  modular source handoff, not the board's complete cottage or landscaping.
