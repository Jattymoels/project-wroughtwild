## Implementation and review

- Separate SOURCE, FINISHED, RUNTIME and REVIEW collections. Seven closed,
  triangulated meshes have one material surface each. Roof courses are backed
  geometric recesses inside the native planes, with exact outer seam boundaries
  and the native 0–2 roof diagonal. The valley retains its two convex native
  collision halves. No overhang or drainage lip intrudes into an adjoining cell.
- [Full dimensions, controls and reconstruction](../../../../tools/wroughtwild-art07/d2/README.md):
  the three coverings are **1 × .5 × 1 m**; the masonry wedge is **1 × 1 × 1 m**;
  girder **2 × .4 × .3 m**; arch **1 × 1 × .25 m**. All remain at native origins
  and poses, including the half-height roof floor offset and full native
  registry occupancy. Blender `(x,y,z)` maps once to Godot `(x,z,-y)`.
- Door address is **1 × 2 × .25 m**, with the native **.94 × 1.94 × .125 m** leaf.
  Recessed joinery, strap edges, blind fastener sockets and finger pull stay
  within that leaf. Existing wall/corner geometry frames it. The native -X
  hinge at **-.5 m**, +.5 m leaf offset and **-90°** open state remain exact.
  The editable REVIEW hinge has a boolean `open` driver; independent reopening
  verifies both poses. Runtime E and save restoration move mesh and collision
  together, with no new animation clock or save field.
- The metal girder preserves a solid box silhouette with an **8 mm** backed web
  recess. The arch preserves the exact twelve-strip visual opening and its
  existing three box colliders. Those colliders are simpler than the visual
  opening: the native visual has some solid surface above the crown where the
  simple body is empty. The visual is checked against the original mesh and
  every collider against native data. This pre-existing distinction is retained,
  not silently repaired or hidden behind a smooth/stone proxy.
- Ordinary construction is unlit. The **6 mm** roof/door joints are physical
  construction detail, not magical scars. Five packed **256 × 256 sRGB**
  provisional colour maps support source inspection; no ORM, normal, emission
  or scar-data maps are introduced. Native Godot family/role materials still
  serve placed pieces, previews and catalogue. Final material integration is
  separate; the legacy metal textures are visibly coarse.
- Source selection: `v02/models-v03`. V01 had excess masonry subdivisions;
  V02 reduced that wedge from 5,708 to **128 triangles** and improved the
  inspection layout. V03 adds only the editable two-state hinge control.
  All seven V03 runtime GLBs are **byte-identical** to tested V02 GLBs.
- Triangle counts: slope **5,084**, hip **4,988**, valley **5,180**, leaf **2,050**,
  wedge **128**, girder **460**, arch **516**; **18,406 total**, **1,552,908 GLB
  bytes**. One source is used at near/middle/far, without an invented LOD budget.
  Five unique source images decode to **1,310,720 bytes**, approximately
  **1.67 MiB** including full RGBA8 mips. Per-GLB embedding can duplicate images;
  actual renderer residency is reported separately.
- Visual limits: repeated course/panel motifs, provisional materials and
  shallow details are less rich than the cottage board. Exact native roof
  endpoints have no thickness available for overhanging shingles. The arch is
  visibly stepped and the girder deliberately lacks a deep I-section opening.

  world rollout or owner visual approval.
