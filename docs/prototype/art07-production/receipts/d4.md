## Implementation and review

- Five distinct growth patterns and palettes, separate cut-end rings, pine
  knots, water-dark bog-oak pores, silvered ash fibres and non-emissive resin
  pockets; over-under reed with opaque backing; irregular coarse cork pores
  and stratified edges. Version `v01` was rejected for overly smooth grain and
  regular cork holes; `v02` is selected. Source recipes and the public mapping
  manifest are in `tools/wroughtwild-art07/d4/`.
- 42 lossless RGB PNG maps: 1024² face and 512² edge sets for each family.
  Albedo is sRGB; +Y tangent normals and ORM are linear. ORM R=255 (no baked AO),
  G=roughness, B=0 (nonmetal). No alpha holes, emission, glow or scar channel.
  Explicit Godot mipmaps and normal-map import settings are retained in the
  ignored handoff; runtime-loaded PNGs must not use default 2D import settings.
- Metric repeats: timber face U/V = 0.5/2 m, cut end = 0.5/0.5 m; reed = 0.4² m;
  cork = 0.3² m. V follows timber member length; reed U follows weft and V stakes.
  Fine pieces crop at the same density. Opposite faces retain tangent handedness.
  Do not apply the old biome tint again. Mapping rules/controls/purposes are in
  `materials.json`, `manifest.json` and the tool README.
- Actual native proxy envelopes: wall 1 × 1 × 0.25 m, fine wall 0.5 × 0.5 ×
  0.125 m, beam 1 × 0.4 × 0.4 m, fine beam 0.5 × 0.2 × 0.2 m, covering panel
  1 × 1 × 0.16 m. Four small covering coupons are explicitly nonplaceable
  inspection samples, not new fine forms. Native reed/cork remain covering-only.
- 26 closed proxy boxes, 312 triangulated faces, no degenerate/nonfinite
  geometry, 14 unique materials / 52 proxy surfaces. Source and imported bounds
  agree with current native data. Blender `(x,y,z)` → Godot `(x,z,-y)`, metres;
  centre origins and board positions are inspection-only. No new lattice pivot
  or collision contract. Near/whole-board views use the same 12 triangles per
  proxy and mipmapped textures; no geometry LOD is necessary for these boxes.
- Roughness/normal-relief controls are art source settings, not game tuning:
  wood 0.78/0.65 mm, pine 0.73/0.40 mm, bog oak 0.66/0.70 mm, ash 0.85/0.85 mm,
  resinheart 0.70/0.50 mm, reed 0.86/1.80 mm, cork 0.91/1.30 mm. Normal relief
  does not create a geometric scar or change collision. There are no new game
  tuning values, costs, currencies, gates or saved fields.
- Compared with the building sheet, colour/grain/weave/pore identities carry
  through. These procedural surfaces are more regular than hand-worked concept
  timber. Finite repeats need per-board UV phase variation on larger walls.
  Physical pegs, chipped corners, lash-bound/inset frames and fitted joinery
  remain the geometry owners' work. Compatibility is brighter and has harder
  shadows than Forward+ under the same neutral light settings; exact renderer
  colour/shadow parity is not claimed. Lower-spec and normal-world appearance
  remain untested. No owner acceptance is inferred from technical checks.
