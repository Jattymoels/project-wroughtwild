## Implementation and review

The copied game's `PieceLook` dispatches chest/fire meshes to E3. Its
`PlacedBlock` attaches a presentation child after native initialization. Those
are the only gameplay-script integration edits, and exist only inside the
copied review. The original hidden proxy and collider retain native behavior.

Chests have a real empty cavity, bottom, fitted boards, backed joins, straps,
corner posts, grips, peens, underside lid battens and alternating hinge knuckles
on a shared pin, with an inset closing lip. The lid follows the identity and open state of the actual
native storage panel. Hardware is included presentation, with no extra iron
ingredient, lock, capacity or traversable cavity. Six units of each family's
native source still create one chest with 960 shared storage units.

The closed native body is 1 × .7 × .8 m, with local Godot bottom −.5 and top .2.
The rear hinge is `(0,.09,−.354)` in Godot and `(0,.354,.09)` in Blender. Export
maps `(x,y,z)` to `(x,z,−y)` once; front is Blender −Y / Godot +Z. There is no
arbitrary scale normalization. The lid opens 78 degrees at 3.8 rad/s, above the
unchanged solid chest body. The existing grid lowers the chest pose another
.15 m, so its lower .15 m intersects the flat review floor. This inherited
seating is explicitly preserved; correcting the ordinary-world rule is outside
this source handoff.

Fire uses the native .8 × .25 × .8 m low body centered at local Y −.125. Six
crossed solid split billets or 29 angular charcoal lumps fit inside it. Timber
has 22 mm geometric V cuts with narrow combustion surfaces 12 mm below the
upper shoulders. These are fuel splits, not magical scars. Ash is separate
opaque geometry visible during the final 30% of the original lifetime.
Native `burn_left`, fuel duration and heat own charring, ember movement and
expiry; pause freezes the presentation. Four timbers cost three fuel units,
burn 45 seconds at heat 1; charcoal costs three, burns 60 seconds at heat 2.
Native expiry removes the object/registry without refund. Existing save policy
omits all live fires; loading does not resurrect fuel or persist ash. The
original one small fire light remains. No flame, smoke or extra heat owner is
introduced.

Materials retain sRGB albedo and linear OpenGL +Y normal/ORM, roughness from ORM
G, and D6 metallic-one binding. Normal strength is .45. Face grain repeats .5 m
across / 2 m along each member; end/metal maps use .5 m. The timber end maps
appear on sawn ends. Explicit PNG imports retain mipmaps and anisotropic
filtering. Shared bindings prevent per-chest texture clones; fuel shaders are
per-instance because ages differ. GLBs also retain embedded source maps, so
this is not a minimal streaming package. All geometry and shaders are opaque;
there is no alpha overdraw or bloom dependency. Ordinary chests emit nothing.

`appearance.json` documents the purpose of seed 42, lid angle/speed/pivot,
4 mm plank joins, 3 mm worked edges, ash threshold, 12 mm ember recess,
emission gain 2 and normal strength .45. These are art controls; no gameplay
tuning is introduced.
