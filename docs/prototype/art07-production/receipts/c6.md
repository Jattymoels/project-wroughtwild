## Implementation and review

Selected geometry is `kit-v06`; final native review is `review-v07`. Nineteen named
variants have 57 near/middle/far exports. The packed master preserves SOURCE,
FINISHED, RUNTIME and REVIEW collections. Earlier versions are diagnostic:
flattened root relief and excessive moss were rejected; coplanar runtime excess
was removed and each export was constrained to the measured native envelope.
Final v07 corrects only the supplementary LOD/emission still captions and lights
those comparisons with the labelled inspection sun. Its complete capture run is
fresh; v06 checks/walks/pulse motion/benchmarks remain applicable and are retained
with their original executed-job records. The adapter, shader, geometry and all
non-capture behaviour are identical. Handoff v01 is superseded by v02.

Weathered stone, timber, quiet metal and soil/litter transitions are paired with
small roots at existing masonry feet. Native walls, cistern, roofs, thresholds,
workbench and paving retain their layout. LF post/clamp/triple-stamp/feed/pointer
parts are directly modelled bevelled versions of their exact existing boxes.
Native labels, marks, source identities and alignment stay authoritative.
One lower old-smithy wall has a recessed accidental-impact scar; later deliberate
containment/collection marks remain orderly and unlit. The scar has no connection
to finite pressure, stock, work, attack or success state.
