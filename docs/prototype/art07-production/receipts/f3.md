## Implementation and review

Selected geometry is `build/art07/f3/v22/assets`, derived from v09 with an explicit
conservative Ventlung far selection. Two individually generated
organic cores were inspected from six directions in material/clay, then finished
in Blender. The apparatus is directly modeled: a braced nodule beside a split
chute with separate trays, and a folded membrane held between boards with guided
platen/hand grip, reed binding and a bored wooden nozzle. The source nodule rests
on a mineral seat beneath its host; the membrane sits in a mineral bowl. Empty
shells contain no stock, interaction, body or yield.

Raw, surface donor, finished master, runtime, device and review collections remain
distinct. Recovery uses a 4 mm voxel surface and coherent source-triangle UV
interpolation. Three tiny disconnected Pullstone components (24 vertices) and
one Ventlung component (8 vertices) were removed before simplification. No raw
source is overwritten. Every final runtime core is one connected closed solid.

Geometric scars have dark broad margins and separate linear R/G/B damaged-margin,
energy and travel data; they preserve original source albedo/ORM. Nominal source
incisions are 35 mm Pullstone / 26 mm Ventlung. BVH rays measure maxima about
34.94 / 25.99 mm before mount scale: source mounts `.70` / `.87`, sorter `.48`,
bellows nonuniform `.70, .40–.52, .70` in Godot XYZ. The same-camera Blender off/peak
renders establish physical damage; native accepted work drives the engine pulse.
There is no shader TIME loop, bloom dependency or scar light. Refresh/reload
does not replay an event and pausing stops travel.

D4 wood face maps are 1024², end maps 512²; D6 iron is 1024², each with albedo,
normal and ORM. Face grain repeats over U .5 m / V 2 m along each member; ends and
iron repeat at .5 m. Albedo is sRGB; ORM/normal/scar data are linear. The two cores
retain their two 2048² source maps each. All 13 images are packed. glTF reports a
sampler-selection warning because the preserved source node graph contains
multiple image references; exported image hashes/maps are inspected separately.
The final build has no mesh-validation repair warning.
