## Implementation and review

- Iron: darkest broad hammer finish, generous domed rivets and an overlapped
  splice. Bronze: warm rounded cast finish, broader collar and protected green
  patina. Steel: tightly dressed cooler finish, narrow joint and hex heads.
  Silver: pale planished finish, small peened pins and two chased seam bands.
  Broad roughness, reflectance and joint geometry distinguish them without neon.
- Selected source: **v03**; textures: **v01**, immutable. v01 Blender candidate
  revealed primitive UV stretching; v02 removed default UV layers. v03 retains
  that geometry/mapping and corrects inspection-coupon depth metadata while
  asserting exactly one UV layer, two material slots and every measured bound.
  Early dim Forward+ review and one misspelled Godot enum are rejected evidence;
  final fresh-copy captures have corrected neutral reflected lighting and no
  engine errors. No assertion was removed or relaxed to pass a bad result.
- Ten objects / **13,608 actual triangles** / **20 material surfaces**, eight
  shared materials. Four girder proxies, four coupons, bronze/silver arch proxies.
  Native envelopes: girder **2 × .4 × .3 m**, arch **1 × 1 × .25 m** (Godot XYZ).
  Coupon front is .5 × .5 m, actual depths .087 m iron, .084 m bronze/silver,
  .0795 m steel. Centre origins, identity scale; Blender `(x,y,z)` maps once to
  Godot `(x,z,-y)`. Review offsets merely stage samples; there are no sockets.
- Physical backed plate gaps, overlapping splice plates and attached pins give
  geometric joint depth. Shallow hammer normals are **not magical scars**.
  Ordinary metals have no emission, pulse clock or source/work-state authority.
  All review controls and geometry dimensions are explained in
  [the source README](../../../../tools/wroughtwild-art07/d6/README.md) and
  `materials.json`. No game tuning was introduced.
- Twelve shared **1024² RGB** PNGs: per-family sRGB albedo, linear OpenGL +Y
  tangent normal and linear ORM (R=1, G=roughness, B=1). Protected seams use
  documented material tint/metallic factors on the **same textures**. UV repeat
  is .5 m, with correct tangent orientation; fine samples crop the same density.
  Godot explicitly binds G/B, enables mipmaps/anisotropic filtering, and replaces
  imported preview surfaces with the shared external bindings.
- Source-only texture arithmetic: 36 MiB RGB, 48 MiB RGBA8 base, **64 MiB full
  RGBA8 mip chain**, independent of object count. PNG file bytes are in the
  texture report/manifest. Total engine counters below include stage, sky,
  render targets and other engine allocations; they are not the source-map cost.
- Same bounded mesh set at near/middle/far; no decimation or new numeric runtime
  budget. Small pins become unreadable at distance as expected; broad finishes
  remain different in the 4/10/24 m views. The maps visibly repeat on long spans.
- **Explicit static limitation:** these are material proxies. Native girder
  collision is a box, and native arches use stepped strips; the smooth arch and
  I-profile here do not replace either. Coupons are not new craftable shapes.
  Iron/steel remain ineligible for arches; silver remains ineligible for doors.
  Fasteners add no component costs. Stateful gameplay is checked separately.
- Compared with the board, this is a neutral studio library rather than the
  weathered cottage composition. Context-specific wear, final attachments and
  full scene adoption remain with geometry/integration owners. Compatibility
  renders brighter than Forward+; grazing silver/bronze highlights and dark
  shaded recesses remain review questions, not certified owner acceptance.
