## Implementation and review

The shrub's normalized original is retained in SOURCE; a separate finished
copy removes the detached 4,312-vertex component and retains the 139,036-vertex
rooted component. Attached sprays supplement coarse generated tips. The
ART-02 tube/leaf method is a task-local derivative, extended into fern pinnae,
curved grass, thorn stems, support-fitted climber, moss tufts and litter chips.
Source, finished, runtime and review collections remain editable.

Raw shrub coordinates subtract (-0.0034347773, 0.0006426573, -0.4208771586),
then multiply by 1.6255600861 for a 1.35 m host. Each simplified LOD reseats
its root at Z=0 (adjustments 1.084 / 4.137 / 20.665 mm). Standard export is
Blender (x,y,z) → Godot (x,z,-y). No second conversion. The climber shares
the support's pivot and fits its actual top surface; it is not an arbitrary
terrain-conforming vine. Near fern height is approximately 0.8 m; moss is
approximately 5 cm and lichen 3 mm high. Individual bounds are audited.

Ordinary materials are quiet and non-emitting. Albedo is sRGB, retained
ORM is linear. Original texture data remains packed and embedded. Opaque
two-sided leaf geometry avoids alpha cards; UV veins and surface variation
add detail. The six assigned ordinary hosts do not introduce scars or pulses.
Pause-aware ambient wind preserves attachment by bending shared coordinates,
18 mm per metre of rooted height at 5.5 s. Ground dress and fitted climber
remain still. The controls in kit.json explain their visual purpose.

The original litter image has a 20.354/255 mean opposite-border difference.
Mirrored coordinate sampling with broad offset/rotated blending removes colour
jumps; the tested boundary-coordinate error is below 9e-16. An explicit mip
chain repairs the first engine candidate's distant shimmer. An authored humus
route, asymmetric pockets and flattened edge plants provide transitions.
Blender's inspection floor is simpler than the engine blend.
