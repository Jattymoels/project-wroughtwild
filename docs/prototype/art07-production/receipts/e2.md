## Implementation and review

The basic forge has a stone plinth, working plate, recessed clinker chamber,
four supported cowl posts, hollow sloped hood/flue and pegged cap. Improved
adds iron cheeks, plate, collar, straps and peened fasteners to that same body.
The shared geometry hash matches between tiers at every detail level.
The broad supported silhouette follows the station concept, within the existing
narrow body. It remains more regular than the illustration: repeated masonry
and iron finish, a single unbranched cut, bright chamber seams, and no added
working door, tools, flame or smoke. These are visible limitations, not accepted
owner feedback.

- Native body remains **.96 × 2 × .96 m**, centre `(0,1,0)`. Basic visible extent
  is **.916 × 1.960 × .905 m**; improved **.919 × 1.960 × .9085 m** in Godot XYZ.
  Floor pivot, identity scale, Blender front **−Y** → Godot **+Z**, export
  `(x,y,z)` → `(x,z,−y)`. Every export fits the unchanged body at all four yaws.
- The working plate retains the `(0,.94,.35)` completion-feedback mount.
  Feeder connection remains the native world-axis centre at **.85 m**, with
  **8 m** range and existing support/obstruction rules. No decorative socket
  can change attachment ownership or direction.
- Left-jamb cut is real geometry: **.038 m width and depth**, energy face
  **.032 m** behind the original skin. Four ray samples per detail level measure
  .038 m depth. Clay renders retain the cut. Runtime forge-only dispatch replaces
  the old forge inlay; every other station keeps its existing inlay.
- Five material roles: Stone, Iron, Soot, Charcoal, Work. Nine packed source maps:
  D5 stone-edge 512², D5 charcoal and D6 iron 1024², each albedo/normal/ORM.
  Albedo is sRGB; OpenGL +Y normals and ORM are linear; roughness reads G.
  Godot shares external maps, with explicit mips and anisotropic sampling.
  Iron metallic 1, soot .3, stone/clinker 0. Source PNGs total **2,934,326 bytes**;
  conservative RGBA8 map residency with full mips is **36 MiB**.
- Stone UVs select one map-block interior per actual stone, avoiding miniature
  masonry courses. This varies texel density with each block. Iron uses .5 m
  metric UVs. No inherited map is repainted or re-attributed as new art.
