## Implementation and review

Selected packed source: `build/art07/b4/v01/kit-final`; selected engine review:
`build/art07/b4/v01/review-budget-v05`, both relative to the worktree above.
The packed master keeps SOURCE, FINISHED, RUNTIME and REVIEW collections with
41 packed images. The recipe imports existing actual models, composes 31 trees,
279 ground plants and 30 rock placements, and exports 26 GLBs. The retained
100×100 m ART-02 ground has 80,000 triangles on its original 0.5 m sampling;
the 3×80 m opaque water strip has 320 triangles at elevation -0.56 m.

The route follows the original ART-02 curve from z=-18 to +18. Metres map once
through glTF as Blender `(x,y,z)` → Godot `(x,z,-y)`; positive Blender Z yaw
maps to positive Godot Y yaw. Original pivots remain, with explicit composition
scale/yaw/burial in `layout.json`; no new sockets. Broadleaf near bounds are about
8.32×7.80×8.57 m and pine 6.48×5.78×9.16 m in Blender XYZ. Shelf envelope is
3.4×2.1×0.85 m. Broadleaf/pine bases bury 0.65/0.55 m before placement scale.

**Canopy fit:** near/middle crowns preserve B1 geometry and attached petioles
(maximum measured root gap 7.61 mm). Their walking-band wood still fails the
existing 0.7×3×0.7 m native body: maximum horizontal radii 2.683 m broadleaf /
2.500 m pine, with 28,036 / 6,656 sampled wood vertices outside it. B4 uses
full-width wood/rock mesh collision only in this isolated review and proves
the open route against it. No native collider was enlarged, no crown was
squeezed into the narrow state-test shape, and ordinary adoption remains blocked
on its own fit decision/context. The review capsule is radius 0.32 m, height
1.8 m, with the camera 1.62 m above its foot.

**Foliage cost:** near/middle/far trees switch at 6/20 m; near broadleaf/pine
have 219,474 / 395,397 triangles, middle 98,032 / 130,597, far 13,800 / 13,297.
Far silhouettes derive from B1's far source and are not attachment masters.
Repeated B2 cover uses its complete far fronds: lush fern 3,924, sparse 2,616,
grass 108/216, moss 3,080, litter 256 and shrub 9,703 triangles. Same-material
fern/talus parts concatenate their actual triangles. Tree families/detail levels,
32 m plant cells and repeated rocks share MultiMesh submissions. Nearby tree
shadows use the far silhouette and a single 26 m shadow view; plant shadows are
omitted. This trades coarse culling and short shadow coverage for lower costs.

**Ground contact:** plants conform per vertex to exact barycentric interpolation
of the retained terrain triangles, including rooted wind. All 279 authored
plant origins are audited; runtime checks directly cover 273 small-plant origins
and 369 terrain ray samples. Maximum collision/interpolation disagreement is
0.00004210 m. Moss/litter remain still. Bank and shelf placements are site-specific
authored overhangs; this is not a general fitter for arbitrary slopes or absent
terrain supports.

Albedo is sRGB; metallic/roughness G/B and scar/wind channels stay linear. An
unused zero R channel is not treated as AO. Nine images are shared by content
hash and limited to 1024 pixels per side, plus the retained floor image with
explicit mipmaps. Every non-image glTF buffer-view byte survives the texture
cook. Engine LOD generation, shadow meshes and attribute compression are disabled.
The Blender finish corrects imported emission/vertex-colour interpretation and
placement yaw without changing selected exported host positions.

Both scars retain physical source displacement: tree **0.0999275 m**, shelf
**0.0644961 m**. Independent nearest-surface distances after export are
0.0722672 / 0.0608314 m; these are different measurements, not claimed as equal
depth. Original topology-matched packed masters were reopened and selected
altered export point sets matched their published sources. Source-master hashes:
tree `bb671f434b1b6021e378c203ab7f8e344b2cbc01c283f5a991ff04ac495672a7`,
shelf `7061684fbc9f52b28715607feefc24f3d96aa92adcbdb7404f5a3780c22b3a7c`.

One explicit pause-aware clock drives attached leaf wind (5.5 s, 0.055 m tip),
rooted plant bend (0.018 m/m), four-second scar pulse and 0.12 m/s broad water
flow. Ordinary surfaces have no magical emission; no shader TIME, bloom or scar
lamps. The copied current quiet synthesis has one voice, 1.6–2.4 s foliage clips,
12–24 s gaps and -36 dB gain, with a separate seeded RNG and pause/mute support.
Automated runs used Dummy audio; listening acceptance is pending.
All scene tuning and its plain-language purposes are in `scene.json` / curated
`controls.json`. No gameplay tuning or accepted design decision changed.

Controls: WASD/mouse; L day/shade/dusk; M scar light; Space pause; Q quiet/mute;
