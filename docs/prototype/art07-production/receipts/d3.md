## Implementation and review

- Separate SOURCE, FINISHED, RUNTIME and REVIEW collections. Each fine export
  has one closed mesh/material surface; each covering has one mesh with closed
  infill and an integral closed frame, separated into two material surfaces.
  Window muntins are unioned into the frame. Every measured vertex is finite;
  all exports have zero degenerate triangles and closed welded topology.
- [Dimensions and controls](../../../../tools/wroughtwild-art07/d3/README.md):
  fine cube .5/.5/.5 m, wall .5/.5/.125, post .15/.5/.15, beam .5/.2/.2,
  slab .5/.125/.5; both coverings 1/1/.16. All origins are centred native poses.
  Blender `(x,y,z)` exports once to Godot `(x,z,-y)` in metres. Native face/edge
  orientation, odd half-metre addresses and full/fine footprints are retained.
- Fine timber has 4 mm wide, 3 mm deep backed joints, a 1.5 mm inward arris,
  metre-based UVs and the D1 quarter-metre board rhythm. Smooth mineral/metal
  alternatives do not inherit plank grooves. Shelf grooves terminate 16 mm
  inside the front/back rim. `settings.json` explains the art controls; there
  is no new gameplay tuning, cost, shape, trait, material or unlock.
- Frames retain native 75 mm window / 45 mm panel widths and 26 mm muntins.
  The 9 mm seating rebate and 2 mm backed frame joints stay inside the existing
  envelope. The complete infill retains native thickness .0384 m; the .0608 m
  difference to the .16 m blocking body is intentional. No hinge or open state
  is invented, and the frame costs no extra piece or resource.
- The copied review installs only a mesh-selection adapter in `piece_look.gd`.
  It preserves native surface 0/infill and 1/frame, original family shaders,
  opaque frames and cinderglass alpha-depth-prepass. Native PieceMesh collision,
  payment, source items, material gates, placement and save code are unchanged.
  All **85 legal pairings** and **48 illegal pairings** retain their rules.
- Two packed 128 x 256 sRGB inspection images support the Blender master;
  colour-buffer conversion was corrected in the selected version. No normal,
  ORM, scar or emission map is added. The native family library supplies actual
  Godot wood, mineral, metal, weave, bark and smoky glass surfaces. These ordinary
  pieces have no magic pulse or scar; recessed joinery is physical craft detail.
- Selected source: **`v01/models-v02`**. V01 exposed a shelf groove that reached
  its rim, losing a narrow side silhouette. The selected source repairs it.
  The rejected version and failure logs remain local. A separate BVH control
  proves that the original slit still fails all four 10-micrometre neighbour
  rays used for triangle-edge precision, while the repaired rim passes all four.
- Master SHA-256:
  `f02214856665525851a0bad564044ab82332b814a6f823ccf96b761c1cf5e46d`.
  Fine timber triangles: cube/wall 198 each, post/beam/slab 76 each; each smooth
