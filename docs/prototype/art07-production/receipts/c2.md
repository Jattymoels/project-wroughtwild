## Implementation and review

Slate has seven actual thin cleaving sheets, open bedding joints and irregular
source-derived edges. Full, worked, last portion and recovered-piece candidates
share the same mineral palette and geometry family. Shellstone retains the
generated ribbed fan and irregular partial/calcified circular sections in its
own textured geometry. It has no tiled medallion pattern. The second orientation
reuses the same source with a half-turn and edge trim; it is not another unique
fossil population. Fine coil detail is softer than the input illustration.

Three deterministic tussocks have green centres, dry margins, attached roots and
a low wind-combed profile. Blades are opaque, two-sided geometry without texture
alpha; their root-weighted wind has no root translation. The 86 composed tufts
keep the authored central approach and quarry working lanes clear.

Selected production sequence: `kit-v04` slate/tussock, generated
`shellstone-generation-v01`, replaced/capped shellstone in `kit-v05`,
`review-v04` and `native-v04`. Earlier jagged/smeared and procedural fossil
candidates were rejected. Their ignored directories remain as diagnostic history;
they are not presented as finished assets. The reconstruction script deliberately
creates the intermediate before replacing all shellstone exports.

Both original quarry picking/collision boxes remain **2.0 × 0.58 × 1.15 m**,
centred at `(0, 0.29, 0)` in Godot. No collider is enlarged. Raw shellstone bounds
in Blender are `(-0.500773,-0.364878,-0.146463)` to
`(0.501445,0.363440,0.147737)`. A single anisotropic fit
`(1.9357066,1.4828684,1.8524799)` reaches `1.94 × 1.08 × 0.545 m`, then a 12 mm
base cut grounds it. Blender Z-up maps once to Godot Y-up on glTF export. Pivot
is ground centre; there are no attachment sockets. Native imported vertices
span slate Y approximately 0–0.529400 m and shellstone −0.000027–0.532750 m.
The tiny negative shell value is reduction tolerance, not a floating base.
Actual original body rays hit Z=0.575 m from 1.425 m away. The real current
player capsule traverses the 1.3 m gap between the two source bodies.

Slate uses seeded periodic mineral albedo and planar face UVs. Shellstone keeps
its source UVs with separate planar UVs on actual cut caps. Ordinary quarry and
grass have no ambient magic emission. B3's altered shelf retains its measured
64.496 mm geometric incision and vertex-mask travel, with the narrow inset
4 s/2.2 peak ambient light separate from native work state. The task-local scar
shader treats the inherited unused ORM red channel as AO=1, following B4's
documented issue. Albedo is sRGB; ORM is sampled as linear data. No bloom/light
node substitutes for the recess. Inherited pine remains distant decorative
context because B4's walking-band canopy/body fit is unresolved; it gets no
replacement collider here.
