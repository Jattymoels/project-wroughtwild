## Implementation and review

- Eight distinct finishes retain gathered-to-worked identity: regular dressed
  beds, irregular dry contacts/lichen, slate laminations, uneven shell fossils,
  varied red-brown firing, fused glossy basalt islands, smoky glazing and porous
  angular charcoal. Ordinary materials have no magical emission or scars.
- 40 closed inspection meshes, 80 exported surfaces, 1,760 actual triangles, zero degenerate or
  nonfinite geometry. Near/middle/far use the same geometry and mipmapped maps.
  These are inspection coupons, not additional legal catalogue forms or a full
  reconstruction of the concept cottage. Slate edges and small bevels are physical.
- Blender metres export `(x,y,z)` → Godot `(x,z,-y)` exactly once. Sample origins
  are centres. Fixed glazing retains its 1 × 1 × 0.16 m envelope, integral
  0.075 m frame, 0.026 m muntins and 0.0384 m pane. Its frame is opaque and the
  entire window blocks movement/projectiles. No opening or separate frame cost.
- 48 RGB PNG maps: eight 1024² face and eight 512² edge sets, each albedo/normal/ORM.
  Albedo sRGB; tangent +Y normals and ORM linear. ORM R=1/G=roughness/B=0.
  Glass alpha 0.72 is a material control; modest normals affect reflections,
  without screen-space refraction. No material pulse or wind exists.
- Exact scale/repeat and art-control purposes are in
  [the D5 recipe README](../../../../tools/wroughtwild-art07/d5/README.md) and
  `materials.json`. Fine pieces crop unchanged physical texel density. Continuous
  courses need a shared metric UV origin. Charcoal stays fuel-only, fieldstone
  rough-only and cinderglass glazing-only. No gameplay tuning is introduced.
- Measured PNG cost: 13,563,044 bytes. Uncompressed RGB base levels: 94,371,840
  bytes; conservative RGBA8 full-mip upper bound: 167,772,160 bytes. Runtime
  residency and fixed-view timing are reported with actual renderer evidence.
- Remaining visual limits: finite repeats remain detectable on long unbroken
  walls; fieldstone is a reusable surface treatment rather than individually
  sculpted rubble. Fossils are shallow impressions. Contact/roof silhouettes
  need their owning module slices. The samples are cleaner and more regular
  than the fully composed concept. Owner acceptance and world adoption remain open.
