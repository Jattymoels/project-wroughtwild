## Implementation and review

one full-width altered source hero, three cork depletion states at three levels,
an inert cut stump, closed recovered resinheart/cork samples, and four unchanged
B2 support families: **23 exports**. The packed editable master separates

**The main unresolved visual limit is the tree footprint.** The full-width
buttressed hero preserves the strongest approved grove ancestry and deep root
wounds, but cannot fit the native tree body. It is labelled source-only and

separate design decision, not an implicit part of this art slice. Crown masses
also remain sparse, repetitive and upright compared with the hanging masses in
the brief. Owner visual review is necessary before ordinary-world adoption.
