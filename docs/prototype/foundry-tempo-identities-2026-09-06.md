# Foundry tempo identities — 6 September 2026

Status: owner-approved implementation slice of the complete Kind identity pass under D-025. The owner reported that Cinder Wake and Afterimage behaved alike, then explicitly approved completing every input. This slice replaces the 24 Quicksilver, Striking Quicksilver and Casting Quicksilver form operations. It retains their IDs, names, compatible skill tags, material costs and base skill deliveries. It introduces no new Kind, skill, economy, save record or discovery gate.

The earlier common Quicksilver pulsing field and common every-third-cast repeats are removed from these form rows. Cinder Wake now requires a positive hit followed by movement, draws one short fire seam and hits each crossing enemy once. Afterimage instead stores a casting position and discharges one packet there when the same skill is recast from elsewhere. Moving alone cannot discharge Afterimage. Neither grants damage to a skill whose native base hit is zero.

The operations dispatch through compiled `identity_tempo_*` properties, not skill or Kind name checks. Native compilation retains damage type, increases/more investment and duplicate ceilings. Authored `*_fraction` values resolve to typed native packets; `*_buildup` values resolve to ordinary and boss chill packets without paying flat buildup twice. Spell-only and attack-only eligibility remains on the authored rows and is also checked by the scene hooks. Damage has no child cast, practice, recovery or recursive contact. Hooks which count contacts require a positive direct hostile hit and a real-input action context; pre-payload contact only records whether the victim was already burning.

All 60 tempo budget/space/timing/presentation values and their plain-language purposes are in the tracked [Foundry tuning](../../data/tuning/foundry.json). Typical single-read damage is 8–10% of the native hit, with explicit 14–18% duplicate ceilings. The operation birth gate is two seconds per player, the opportunity window is 3.2 seconds, only one live memory of each operation is allowed, and the total node ceiling is 24. The three-hit/three-position sequences count real casts, rather than enemies in a cleave or duplicated callbacks. No event refreshes its own unspent lifetime. Separate real casts can seed a new opportunity once the old one is spent and its birth gate has elapsed. Conditional movement, pursuit and mote opportunities cannot pay at or after expiry; scheduled spell marks and completed stationary afterglows retain their once-only terminal release.

The movement threshold is 1.2 metres. Short lines are bounded at six metres and explicitly reject solid cover. The fire seam is grounded without changing terrain or collision. Returns collide with cover and can expire. Push and stagger use quarter strength against bosses; chill retains native boss rules. The two armour rewards share the existing strongest personal cast-armour pool and last 1.8 seconds. Clearing consumes exactly one active control in root, harry, mark order. Cooldown rewards reduce actual remaining time without storing credit. All live memories are transient and use the `foundry_tempo` death/load cleanup group; ordinary player, gear and save formats remain unchanged.
