# ART-07 — a full frontier and a coherent building kit

**ART-07A design pass delivered, 9 September 2026; new boards proposed for visual review.** The owner asked to start
designing all nature assets and the world's feel against the original wide
landscape reference, together with all craftable placeable pieces. Their
“Perfect” also visually approves the preceding [ART-06C deep lifelines and
porcupine face](roster-lifelines-2026-09-09.md). Rigs and native creature adoption
remain separate unfinished work.

## Outcome and scope

Deliver original environment/building concept boards, a complete inventory of
existing construction shapes and craftable kits, individual production briefs,
and a staged production order. Nature includes canopy, understory, ground,
water margins, geology, resources, ruins, wildlife habitat and distant scenery.
“All” means coverage of the current game catalogue and the environmental roles
needed to dress it; it does not claim an unlimited final-game asset library.

Affected systems: art direction, asset catalogue and art production queue.
D-013/D-030 govern the transformed natural world; D-017/D-018/D-021/D-032
govern construction, materials and retained geography; D-029/D-031 and the
Living Frontier work items govern physical devices and resource ownership.
The existing construction/crafting/worldgen tables and engine bounds are read
as constraints. This pass changes no gameplay tuning, generation or saves.

Assumptions: retain the approved boar/wolf/stag/moth and six new animal
ancestries, the ART-02 grove language and all four approved ART-04 families.
New silhouettes and habitat dressing in these boards are proposals for review.
Additional passive species, ecological simulation, new furniture recipes,
larger building footprints and new era mechanics require separate decisions.
There is no missing decision that prevents this design pass.

Small plan: inspect the supplied reference and current catalogue; design a
coherent environment keyframe and family boards; specify every existing
placeable and resource plus supporting nature roles; verify coverage and
provenance; publish this design slice. Mesh production follows in bounded kits.

## The world should feel inhabited

[ENV-002](../art/references/environment/env-002-dense-frontier-original.png)
gets its fullness from overlapping canopy masses, foreground detail, layered
rock and distant haze. Preserve those compositional qualities, not its exact
geography or branded image content. The new keyframe is original concept art,
not a game capture or a promise that the present renderer can draw every leaf.

Build a walk with several scales of interest: distant treelines and broken
skyline; mid-distance trunks, rock shelves and ruins; nearby ferns, grasses,
roots and litter. Trees should form connected irregular masses with visible
trunks beneath crowns. Leave intentional paths, workshop clearings and combat
sightlines. The ground beneath a crown should belong to the tree through roots,
shade, leaf fall and moisture, rather than a tree sitting on a blank plane.

The five existing biomes keep their identities. Meadow edges feel sheltered but
readable; Rootvault is tall and layered; the fen is full of reeds, roots and
wet banks; Glasswind/Shellcut uses tough low plants and fossil rock shelves;
the wastes uses deadwood, broken stone and pockets of surviving scrub. Fullness
does not require equally dense tree planting in all five places.

Augmentation changes host structure. A split trunk has pulled-open wood and
deep dark channels running from root through the wound, with energy visible
inside. A rock fracture has matching depth, not a glowing line painted over an
intact surface. Most ordinary vegetation remains unlit. Strong local scars are
discoveries, and later physical growth must preserve approved era boundaries.
White/Red/Blue/Green retain their accepted material and device meaning; Blue
does not turn an entire region into ice by colour association.

Wildlife belongs in habitat pockets, not a uniform field of extra enemies.
Reuse the approved animals with clear resting/foraging/perching compositions
for art review. Those poses are animation briefs, not new AI behaviour or spawn
rules. Existing host and pack locations remain authoritative at integration.
Quiet motion can come from leaves, reeds and water. Audio should have local
events and quiet gaps; it must not reintroduce the continuous ambient drone.

## A home made from the same world

Building families should express the material collected: shell fossils survive
in dressed shellstone; slate cleaves into thin layers; bog oak has dense dark
grain; reed panels show weaving; cork has a porous cut edge; cinderglass is
smoky with a complete integral frame. Ordinary walls do not acquire magical
emission just because they stand near a source.

Construction uses exact lattice forms and material eligibility, not separate
freeform sculptures for every wall. Use shared material surfaces and accurate
Blender modules. Fine pieces preserve their existing dimensions and costs.
Native dimensions, placement preview, body and rotation must agree, including
corner fits, roof seams and window blocking. Concept sheets are not dimensioned
manufacturing drawings. A decorative feature that breaks fit is redesigned
inside the existing envelope; collision is not ignored to make it fit.

Station workfaces stay compact and distinct. A forge upgrade changes the same
forge appearance in place; it is not a fifteenth kit. The seven ordinary
fixtures expose their real components: luminous heart, tension drum, receiving
cradle, resonator, sorting nodule, membrane and separate feeder supplies.
ART-04's four coloured source/material/device families are reused, not replaced
by new concept inventions. State visuals read native ownership: neither a pulse
nor an attractive glowing chamber can imply free energy, extra stock or work.

## Production method and acceptance

Use the established high-quality concept → local TRELLIS → Blender inspection
route for organic hero shapes where it helps. Trees still need deliberately
attached branch/canopy systems and wind-ready foliage; terrain materials need
tiling and seam review. Use direct Blender modelling for grid pieces, frames,
moving mechanisms and collision-sensitive parts, with generated organic cores
only where useful. Do not use an image-to-3D scene containing a whole cottage
as the modular building kit.

Every production source needs scale/pivot, material separation, actual geometry
for important scars, full/worked state where applicable, collision-independent
render detail, distance variants and packed editable source. Measure cost in an
isolated game view before ordinary adoption. The dense ART-02/05 source costs
are not approved runtime budgets. Set numeric budgets after the first measured
kit; this design pass introduces no game tuning values.

Acceptance of this pass is complete coverage and reviewable designs, not model
generation or a full-world makeover. The [concept gallery and image review](../art/concepts/environment/2026-09-09-frontier/README.md)
and [complete asset catalogue](../art/concepts/environment/2026-09-09-frontier/asset-catalogue.md)
are the review artifacts. Runtime claims must wait for their own implementation
evidence.

## Delivered and checked

Five original boards cover the landscape keyframe, six habitat vignettes,
building/material families, stations/home pieces and seven ordinary fixtures.
The station sheet's first framing cropped its chimney tops; the second version
corrects this, retaining the first image and edit ancestry for provenance.
All four approved ART-04 coloured devices are included through their existing
handoffs. New individual model sheets and production remain open.

The catalogue/check tool confirms 26 shape briefs, 19 material briefs, 273
legal material/shape pairings, all 14 craftable/native kit mappings, one forge
upgrade, all 22 worldgen resource node types, 33 supporting environmental art
roles and all 16 current overworld actor/host IDs. It reads actual station and
fixture bounds. Ordinary profiles expose ten kits; the four coloured additions
keep their supported-LF-profile filter. Recipe costs/unlocks are recorded but
not changed.

Image validation passes for six intact PNGs (five selected boards plus one
superseded framing), six exact submitted prompts, six unchanged local generated
original copies, one verified edit parent and the unchanged ENV-002 reference.
All 23 local links in the new gallery/catalogue/work-item/tool documentation
resolve. Catalogue freshness/coverage checks pass. No gameplay checks were
run: this slice contains no runtime, tuning or save change. There are no new
game tuning parameters or approved numeric performance budgets.

Remaining visual limitations are recorded beside each board: exact module
boundaries, fine pieces, compact station protrusions, Thrumroot fibre identity
and feeder socket/output details require individual production review. The
keyframe's landscape and animal staging are illustrative. No full-world
performance, rig, collision-fit or source-depletion implementation is claimed.
