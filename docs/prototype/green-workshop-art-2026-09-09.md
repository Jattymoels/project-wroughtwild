The isolated native suite passes **111 checks**, plus **13 checks in a fresh
process**: fixed source stock/rare-only ownership, partial/full carrying,
independent branch and receiver refusals, exact paid escrow/cargo/progress,
pending Blue pause/cancel/rewire, duplicate/cycle refusal, and no request replay
after winding/reload. The actual remaining source lots contain the rare Preserving
claim; no material grant, artificial inventory consumption or outcome rewrite is
needed for this coverage. Native save and world rules are unchanged.

Blender passes **133 checks** across reimported runtime exports and reopened
packed source. Source details use 35,999 / 15,999 / 7,999 triangles. The complete
junction uses **1,998 triangles across four surfaces**, including 1,380 housing
and 618 resin triangles. The already-small device keeps that geometry at all
three exports. Individual recovered pieces use 190 / 184 / 244 triangles.
Source/housing use 2k maps; each resin piece uses its own 1k maps.

The source remains within 1.5 × 1.1 × 1.5 m, and the junction within
0.65 × 1.18 × 0.55 m, including the original 1.18 m terminal. Reimport checks
prove closed recovered volumes, ground pivots, actual stem-to-cradle seating,
both physical stem-to-branch joins, UVs and nonempty luminous cores. The generated
host remains an exterior mesh; it is not certified watertight. New resin interior
is authored inference, not a new cutting simulation or inventory family.

Forward+ and Compatibility each pass **1,126 visual/state checks with 132
captures**, including actual source/claim/spent/rare-only states, three detail
levels in day/shade, independently lit branches, no unpaid work, held paid jobs,

is decoded and compared against every original engine frame and its duration.
The final evidence suite passes **419 checks**; its 96 source frames retain the
complete 12,000 ms timeline and exact pixels in the 12,710,574-byte WebP.
