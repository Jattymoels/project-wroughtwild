## Creative contract to settle

Produce one concise generation/place specification with:

- Explicit determinism: same seed and saved generation version, same geography; different seeds vary underlying country, biome layout, impact/influence reach and useful approaches. Authored rules and forms are not a fixed map.
- A four-force contract table: physical effect, eligible biome/host, terrain response, growth/fauna relationship, visual/motion cue, actual player opportunity and non-effects. Define all four now, implement the first selected case in LAND-02.
- Influence enters before final terrain/habitat composition. Show the dependency from country and host through impact/influence to present landform and recovery; a final tint/scatter pass is insufficient.

- The pre-impact valley/workshop relationship; displacement and altered host; surviving/recovered growth; source use. Do not invent the meteor sender, exact date or intentional ancient leyline installation.
- The arrival, first reveal, two route choices, close discovery and return view. Specify what the player can infer at each point without a lore dump.
- Foreground, middle-distance and skyline roles. Reserve canopy openings and continuous ground/plant edges. Provide a practical response to RF-09's dark bank and unreadable impact.
- The two home opportunities: scenic dry expansion shelf versus sheltered grove work area. Preserve four existing radius-14 m cores globally, existing costs and opening safety. No site buffs, free structures or compulsory plots.
- Gallery Woodland's physical conditions, tall canopy/roots/understorey pattern, transition into existing meadow/fen, appropriate current fauna and quiet sound/motion. It must differ structurally from existing Deep Forest.
- White's form/timing/host expression with colour as reinforcement. Distinguish the ordinary finite pressure owner from later LF White mineral/request semantics. First-slice fauna keeps existing behavior; any LF host adoption is named separately.
- Three visible success statements: a recognisable recovered landmark at player height; a useful source reached through meaningful routes; a setting that suggests a specific home/workshop design. Functional assertions cannot establish these.

Treat LAND-00's 250–350 m span and 20–35 m relief as proposed starting ranges. Revise them to fit the actual 1,024 × 1,024 × 96 terrain and usable paths, rather than copying concept proportions literally.

1. **New saved geography.** Recommend the specific successor profile/revision, separate inputs, profile allowlists and explicit New World selection. Freeze V1–V8 and LF Continue. 2. **Shared place description.** Identify the minimum fields linking impact direction, ridge/basin shape, lake, influence identity, host, recovery patches, source, home/route and view reservations. Reuse current records where appropriate; no general ecosystem framework. Differentiate new biomes from V8's three discovery regions/five rare sites.
3. **Real fissure contact.** Choose a bounded dry native recess outside lake contact, with visible floor and usable escape/approach, supported by the shared terrain mesh/collision/sampling/edit path. Establish initial width/depth/lip targets and how irregular art joins survive digging and paid construction. Explain why the cut reads as depth; do not hide a surface strip under an opaque terrain face.
4. **Seed composition and fallback.** Write a concrete placement order with typed influence and host response before final landform, water and recovery, plus a small set of allowed variations. Specify what remains guaranteed if lake/cave/source/home constraints conflict. A fallback must retain the source payoff and place identity.
5. **Art kit.** Select the minimum authored ridge/fracture, two tree silhouettes and bank/root/ground joins. List reuse versus new forms, scale, contact, material and export requirements. Plan shared resource preparation at real world entry. No new paid service, package or engine change.
6. **Later acquisition boundary.** If the owner chooses LF vocabulary adoption, list the exact existing source/recipe/device/host dependencies that later LAND-03/04 can reuse independently of LF campaign/labs. Preserve work/renewal/cost semantics. This is a dependency note, not permission to add those systems to LAND-02.

The first implementation must include art and landform together. A short feasibility step may be embedded in that implementation for the actual fissure/contact risk; do not replace this plan with a broad terrain rewrite, technical audit or another research task.
