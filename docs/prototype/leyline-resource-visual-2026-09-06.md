# Leylines and rare resources: fractured hosts, contained light

**Human-approved graphics work item, 6 September 2026; D-013/D-030/D-031.**
The owner requested this pass while playtesting and supplied an environmental
reference: weathered ground, irregular branching black fissures, narrow buried
blue-white light and small luminous deposits. The character and interface in
that reference are excluded. This request brings forward a bounded graphical
pass after the earlier acceptance of the current finish for now.

## Outcome and assumptions

Existing leylines should read as fractures left by violent augmentation, and
the five existing rare resources should look worth approaching and inspecting.
Dark host material and small contained light carry the reference into the
frontier's established earthy daylight. Lanternheart, Thrumroot, Stormglass,
Pullstone and Ventlung retain distinct organic/mineral identities.

The old blacksmith's ruin predates the catastrophe. Its pressure is accidental;
the newly built player machinery remains the only deliberate containment.
Decorative traces do not imply renewable energy, additional harvestable stock
or a functioning pre-existing production network.

Affected systems are the Godot trace presentation, shared rare-resource art,
harvest/pressure visual state and their art resources. Native simulation,
generation profiles and inputs, terrain, source IDs, colliders, interaction,
recipes, yields and save contracts retain authority. The fractures are shaded
surface geometry, not excavated terrain or new traversal obstacles. This pass
does not resolve the separate timber-demolition conflict.

## Implementation sequence

1. Capture the current V5 world at fixed daylight/dusk cameras and record warmed
   frame costs before replacing live art paths.
2. Replace exposed trace strips with grounded, tapered, irregular dark channels,
   chipped lips, fine branches and narrow intermittent light. Respect native
   buried/broken intervals and building/excavation suppression.
3. Refine authored rare-resource hosts with shared rough material treatment,
   fine local filaments and restrained core motion. Visual stock/work state
   must survive refresh and depletion; the old hearth uses its native ledger.
4. Review matched views, finite harvesting and pressure state, plus relevant
   existing interaction/save regressions. Investigate matched median/p95
   regressions exceeding ten percent.

Reuse the approved local authored meshes and native shaders. Shared materials,
bounded mesh detail and existing distance culling control rendering cost.
No new packages, asset services, light networks or per-frame geometry rebuilds.
Each new art tuning field explains its visual purpose in its resource script.
The owner's running playthrough and normal save must not be interrupted;
review instances and outputs are isolated under ignored `build/` paths.

## Acceptance and evidence

- At player height, fissures have an irregular dark mouth and thin internal
  light rather than a wide uniform luminous stripe; branches taper naturally.
- All five resources retain recognisable hosts and readable work areas, with
  contained light visible in daylight and restrained at dusk.
- Partial work, remaining stock, depletion and highlight agree after refresh.
  Art rebuilds do not change resource identity, world state or source pressure.
- Surface details remain grounded, hidden beneath buildings and broken across
  unsupported terrain; native exposure gaps and legacy worlds remain intact.
- Actual matched captures and targeted test/performance results are recorded.
  Review scripts use a separate process and isolated outputs while the owner
  plays. Concurrent GPU load is reported if it limits timing comparisons.
