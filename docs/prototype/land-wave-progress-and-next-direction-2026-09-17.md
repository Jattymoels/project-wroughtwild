# LAND progress, playtest feedback and proposed next direction

## Latest owner feedback

After LAND-05 integration the owner reports that generation and the world feel
much better from initial looks and play. This is positive initial aesthetic/
experience feedback, not a completed campaign, balance or performance assessment.
They will continue playtesting. Keep subsequent observations together in the
coordination sheet; no worker is automatically launched from a note.

The owner proposes a future probability-driven composition layer: weighted
likelihoods for which biome comes next, the chance of meteor influence by colour,
and very rare occurrences of multiple colours merging. They want to develop what
each combination does to land, fauna, stone and other hosts, making varied cases
exciting to discover. Augmentation/enhancement is the common cause; each colour
heightens existing properties differently. This is recorded future direction,
not implemented distribution, selected numerical probabilities or new mixed-force
gameplay rules. Do not describe current V13 as already having this system.

## Recommended next sequence — proposed, not dispatched

1. **Finish this playtest round.** Consolidate actual observations; address any
   concrete progression/save blocker first. Reopen performance work on relevant
   evidence, not a speculative benchmark wave. No automatic additional LAND slice.
2. **Design one biome × influence × host catalogue and generation contract.**
   For each supported case record the property enhanced, visible terrain/stone/
   plant/fauna expressions, source/device payoff, habitat constraints, rarity and
   production readiness. Unknown combinations stay explicitly unimplemented.
   Separate base-biome placement/adjacency weights from conditional impact-colour,
   strength and rare-overlap weights. Give uninfluenced/recovered ground an explicit
   place in the distribution. Geography, neighbour suitability and coherent
   regions should constrain the weights; a random roll alone cannot compose land.
3. **Prove the weighted rules with a small supported subset.** Reuse existing
   biomes/forces and deliver a few meaningful single-colour host relationships
   before expanding combinations. Validate varied ordinary seeded worlds, readable
   transitions, useful discovery and honest save identities. Keep unsupported
   combinations out of selection rather than disguising them with tint.
4. **Add one deliberately designed rare two-colour interaction.** Decide whether
   it is overlapping effects or a new combined interaction, how it alters hosts,
   how the player recognises its rarity and what existing/new payoff is intended.
   Any new gameplay needs its own explicit design decision. Then expand the
   catalogue in small batches, guided by playtest value and art effort.

Compose reusable host rules where that produces distinct results; author special
exceptions where a memorable discovery needs them. Avoid requiring every biome ×
colour × species × mixture up front. Exact percentages, pairings, budgets, slice
count and IDs for the next wave are not selected. Continue coordinator discipline:
scope the next wave once playtest notes are consolidated, preserve old saves,
commit reviewable work and let the owner start implementation sessions.
