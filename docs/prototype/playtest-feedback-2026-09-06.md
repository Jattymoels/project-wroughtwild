# Melee, crafting and visual quality — owner playtest feedback

Owner feedback, 6 September 2026, following the all-input Foundry pass. This
records observations separately from proposed balance or art changes.

## Observations

- Melee was tough but rewarding. The trial's structure made sense, but its
  encounters still felt too easy and the character did not yet feel like a
  recognisable build. The exact loadout, trial/tier, clear time and whether the
  session had loaded the latest Foundry runtime were not supplied.
- World and crafting progression were confusing. The owner has not yet fully
  explored the stations or structure building. The concrete menu preference is
  to show the relevant bench's recipes and put available crafts first.
- The current graphics are below the desired finish. The owner described an
  old Newgrounds-like impression and asked about art direction versus Blender
  tooling. Earlier temporary acceptance is not acceptance of this as final art.

## Crafting clarification

Apply current-station recipe filtering and craftable-first ordering using the
existing native recipe preview. Preserve costs, skills, availability checks,
station families/upgrades and progression. This is a presentation correction,
not a new crafting rule or recipe gate. Relevant material dependencies can still
explain where to obtain an ingredient without filling every bench with the
entire recipe catalogue.

## Proposed next combat review

The feedback is evidence for an encounter/build review, not a calibrated global
life or density multiplier. Compare the same legal melee build before and after
a deliberate coherent Foundry arrangement in the same encounter. Verify that
the latest runtime is loaded. Observe clear coverage, recovery decisions, target
priority, boss exposure and how often the selected operations actually trigger.

Then adjust encounter composition, concurrent pressure and reinforcement timing
where that comparison shows a gap. Preserve the accepted 24-enemy concurrent cap
and two-major-hazard ceiling. More enemy life alone can lengthen a fight without
making the build's decisions more apparent. No combat tuning changes are approved
or implemented by this feedback record. Existing automated cohort measurements
remain limited evidence, especially after the Foundry identity changes.

## Proposed visual target

Use a small playable place to establish the desired finish before another broad
asset expansion: surviving woodland, an asteroid-struck pre-cataclysm smithy,
a short leyline approach and one rare-resource discovery. This uses existing
lore and content roles. It does not add a biome, technology or resource rule.

Gather a few owner-selected game/art references, specifying what to borrow from
each: shape, surface treatment, lighting, vegetation, characters and motion.
Current weathered-frontier/daylight and accidental-augmentation rules remain
authoritative until revised. Do not infer a photorealistic, permanently dark or
higher-polygon target from dissatisfaction alone.

Iterate modelling and natural silhouettes, material grain/roughness, layered
vegetation, lighting/contact shadows, atmospheric depth and motion together.
Review at normal player height, in movement and combat, with daylight/interior
comparisons and matched performance measurements. One attractive Blender render
or a test count is not visual acceptance. Additional MCP connections are useful
only if a concrete authoring capability is missing from the existing pipeline.

This is a proposal awaiting reference direction, not an approved full art rebuild.
