# LAND-05 heightened host kit

Original direct Blender geometry, not image-to-3D. Recipe:
`tools/wroughtwild-land05/build_hosts.py`. Editable master:
`D:/Wroughtwild/work/land05-closeout/build/land05/source-art/force-hosts.blend`.
`source.json` records every selected GLB hash and bounds; units are metres.

The existing LAND04 entry preparer retains these original meshes and shared
materials before controls release in V12/V13. Green has broad flattened woody
junctions feeding tall attached woodland fronds and small low dry-host fans.
Short shared bark links follow the source approach and branch into the resin
workplace. The Steppe network clips triangles at its two native dry breaks.
White repeats asymmetric loaded roots and offset plates; Blue uses overlapping
cupped sheaths above thin retained deposits. No tint/scale-only substitute.

Native terrain owns the substantial banks and contact/picking. Woody basal
skins remain shallow (about 0.2 m or less); tall flexible fronds are foliage,
not a new solid trunk. No extra invisible collision shell or resource owner.
Every conformed vertex checks current support. Existing paid-building masks
hide intersecting pieces; ordinary chunk rebuild after digging removes roots
whose original supporting cells have gone. Unrelated old profiles do not gain
these journey patches. Settings explain clearances and connection spacing.

The prior LAND04 master and recipe remain intact for provenance. These forms
are deliberately stylised. They do not redesign the approved Scarwater cliffs,
change source outputs or introduce growth simulation.
