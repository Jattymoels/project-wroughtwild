# LAND-03 dry living country: original art

The six runtime forms are original Blender geometry authored for this slice.
Boards 03/04 inform low horizontal colonies, tough susceptible growth and swollen
release recesses. No concept pixels, external art, image-to-3D model or paid
service was used. This is directly authored procedural Blender art; polygon
count and a successful export are not claims of visual reference parity.

Recipe: `tools/wroughtwild-land03/build_steppe.py`, Blender 4.5.9. Shared repository
`tools/wroughtwild-art07/b2/geometry.py` supplies only the original low-level mesh,
leaf and tube helpers. Art recipe seed 170903 plus role offset 311 regenerates
exact selected forms. Each mesh retains vertex root/tip and living/dry material
variation. Standard glTF coordinates/metres are used. `source.json` records
triangles, dimensions and exact runtime hashes.

Editable master:
`D:/Wroughtwild/work/land03-dry-steppe/build/land03/source-art/steppe-production.blend`.
Runtime: `game/land03/assets/`. No source master, DLL, cache or import artifact
needs to be committed to preserve the reproducible recipe.

The broad ribs, cuts and source-bearing bank are native generated terrain with
shared render/contact/edit ownership. The small cosmetic joins create no hidden
walls or stock. `cover.gd` follows the actual biome and shared native host points;
seeded connected patches vary density and form. It reserves native routes, real
source workplaces and owned sites. Shrubs and mineral joins leave existing home
cores clear; low grass can occupy empty cores until paid building suppresses it.
Each full footprint checks
native unbroken support, water and actual surface triangles. Chunk rebuilding
removes unsupported growth after digging. Existing paid-building suppression
uses exact world transforms and mesh bounds. No second save ledger is created.

`kit.gd` retains meshes/materials during terrain entry before initial streamed
chunks. Movement arrivals and terrain refresh reuse that cache. V11 alone binds
the actual ecological mask for a warm, dry soil/mineral material response;
Scarwater continues using its approved V10 surface/material/tree paths. No
appearance change is applied to V10 or older Continue.

`settings.json` explains every placement tunable alongside its value. At the
revised authored settings, 2 m growing points support connected low mats with
18 m patches, 0.48 to 0.94 occupied points, occasional 12% sage and local 35% Red
brush at eligible hosts. Cover ends at 112 m; native landforms carry the distance.
Seeded root offsets across a 0.55 m span break planting rows. Low grass may grow in
empty home cores and tests root-only finite-node clearance; full support and paid
construction suppression still use its actual footprint. Roots embed 4.5 cm and reject
unsupported 1.1 m rises or 0.4 m plane mismatch.
These are composition/support values, not new gameplay rules.

Material values in `host.gdshader` use a 1.65 linear colour gain, low wrapped
foliage/backlight and small local Red emission only on authored susceptible
nodes. `ground.gdshaderinc` blends ochre dry soil, olive growth and warm mineral
inside the V11 Steppe biome mask; it does not paint blanket red terrain.

The first actual reveal exposed overly sparse isolated mats and smooth mineral
banks. The second composition revision reduces low-grass-only finite-node
clearance, adds seeded root jitter and preserves the inherited mineral texture
on rib faces instead of washing it out with Steppe soil colour. Native landform
refinement is recorded separately in the main result.

The revised ordinary reveal, source approach and sheltered hollow were visually
inspected at player height. Connected straw/olive growth bands, low sage, exposed
mineral caps and long shallow passages now establish a country distinct from
Scarwater. The wide source picture confirms an open workplace but does not alone
prove close Red-pocket readability. It is not a claim of full reference parity
or owner aesthetic acceptance.

Known art limits: broad bare slopes and some repeated grass/fringe outlines
remain; outer native slopes can show scalloped steps. Small cosmetic clasts have
no standalone collision and low cover only fills loaded terrain. Actual use,
close host detail and final evidence are recorded in the main LAND-03 result.
